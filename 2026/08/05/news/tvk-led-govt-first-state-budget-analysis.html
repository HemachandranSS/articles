STATE BUDGET · TAMIL NADU POLITICS

Amid high expectations, TVK-led govt. set to present its first State Budget today

The Cabinet has already discussed the broad contours of 436 proposals from various departments under the ‘Vetri Tamilagam Vision Document’.

By N. Sai Charan · CHENNAI

Amid heightened expectations, the first Budget of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK)-led coalition government in Tamil Nadu, headed by Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay, will be presented in the Assembly on Wednesday. Finance Minister N. Marie Wilson will present the State Budget for 2026-27 at 10 a.m.

The Cabinet has already discussed the broad contours of 436 proposals from various departments under the government's ‘Vetri Tamilagam Vision Document’. Organised under the Thirukkural-inspired themes of Aram, Porul, and Inbam, as in the TVK's election manifesto, the document outlines several of the party's key poll promises for implementation. Many of these proposals are expected to find a place in the Budget.

“Organised under the Thirukkural-inspired themes of Aram, Porul, and Inbam, the document outlines key poll promises for implementation.”

White Paper
The TVK-led government had released a White Paper on the State's finances flagging several shortcomings and irregularities in financial management under the previous regime. The White Paper said Tamil Nadu's actual outstanding debt stood at ₹13.18 lakh crore, including the combined debt of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs). It also flagged a “revenue collapse” and highlighted the declining share of the State's Own Tax Revenue (SOTR) compared with that of several other States.

The government has also constituted a Revenue Augmentation Committee under the chairmanship of economist Montek Singh Ahluwalia to recommend measures for the sustainable augmentation of the State's tax and non-tax revenues through improved compliance, plugging leakages, rationalising tax rates, fees and exemptions, and identifying new and underutilised sources of revenue.

Against this backdrop, the Budget assumes added significance as the TVK had made several promises on the welfare front ahead of the 2026 Assembly election and is now expected to outline its roadmap for fulfilling them. Many of the party's poll promises require substantial financial resources for their implementation.

Meanwhile, since July 2, Mr. Vijay has been holding a series of department-wise review meetings to assess the implementation of ongoing infrastructure projects and welfare schemes across departments. The outcome of these reviews is expected to be reflected in the Budget proposals. According to sources, there will be some important announcements for youth and women.

Since assuming office on May 10, the TVK-led government fulfilled some of its poll promises. These include the formation of the Singappen Special Force to prevent crimes against women and children, providing 200 units of free electricity to domestic consumers whose bi-monthly consumption is up to 500 units, administrative sanction for an Anti-Narcotic Drugs Unit, and waiver of cooperative crop loans up to ₹75,000.

The State government also issued an order to implement the Thaaimaman Thanga Mothiram Thittam, under which one-gram gold rings will be provided to babies born in government hospitals across the State. It also ordered the expansion of the Perunthalaivar Kamarajar Breakfast Scheme (previously called the CM's Breakfast Scheme) to include students in government and government-aided schools from Class VI to VIII. Both schemes will be launched in September.

The Assembly proceedings during the Governor's Address and the motion of thanks to the Governor's Address were telecast live during the previous session. The Budget session is also expected to be telecast live.

Key highlights from the TVK manifesto include collateral-free education loans up to ₹20 lakh, stipend-based internships for 5 lakh youth, cultivator rights cards, and abolition of peak-hour electricity charges for MSMEs.
