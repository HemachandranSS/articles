INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS · UKRAINE · RUSSIA

No compromise with Ukraine, Putin indicates to U.S. envoys during talks

The Russian President proposes a short-term ceasefire in key frontline city in Donetsk; he also rules out agreeing to foreign troops being stationed in Ukraine; Kushner, Witkoff urge Putin to include European representatives in next round of talks

Agence France-Presse

MOSCOW  |  November 13, 2026

Vladimir Putin indicated that Russia would not compromise on hardline demands to end the Ukraine offensive, refusing to water down territorial claims over areas Moscow says it has annexed.

Meeting Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff at the Senate Palace of the Kremlin after two days of talks in Abu Dhabi, he proposed a short-term ceasefire in a key frontline city in Donetsk, ruled out foreign peacekeeping troops in Ukraine as a “continuation of war,” and rejected demilitarised or “grey” zones between the sides.

The U.S. envoys urged him to include European representatives in the next round. European and Kyiv leaders have warned against a “new Yalta.” Kushner and Witkoff were due to travel to Dublin to meet President Volodymyr Zelenskyy at a European summit.
