No need for Opposition approval to launch strikes, says Israel’s Katz

Agence France-Presse

JERUSALEM  |  November 13, 2026

Israeli caretaker Defence Minister Israel Katz said on Thursday that the outgoing coalition does not need permission from the Opposition to decide on fresh military operations, following calls to show restraint.

“We do not need approval from the Opposition or any legal authority” for the Army to carry out strikes, he said.
