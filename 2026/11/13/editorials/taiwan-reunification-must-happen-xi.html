Taiwan ‘reunification’ must happen, says Xi

Reuters

BEIJING  |  November 13, 2026

Chinese President Xi Jinping said the “tide of history cannot be stopped” and that complete reunification of Taiwan with the “motherland” must be realised and will certainly be realised.

Speaking at Beijing’s Great Hall of the People on the 160th birthday of Sun Yat-sen, he pledged peaceful reunification and “one country, two systems” under the one-China principle, and called on Chinese people including those in Taiwan to oppose independence “separatist” forces. He made no mention of using force in this speech.

Taiwan’s government rejects China’s claims, and no major Taiwanese party backs “one country, two systems.” China has never officially renounced force and continues daily military operations around the island.
