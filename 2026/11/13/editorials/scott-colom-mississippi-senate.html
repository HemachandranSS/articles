Democrat Scott Colom elected in Mississippi’s closely contested race

Associated Press

JACKSON  |  November 13, 2026

Democratic nominee Scott Colom defeated Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi’s closely contested U.S. Senate race after prolonged ballot counting.

He becomes the state’s first Black U.S. Senator since post-Civil War Reconstruction. The last time Mississippi chose a new Democratic Senator was 1947.
