Yemen’s Houthis say they targeted Saudi East-West pipeline, Yanbu

Agence France-Presse

RIYADH  |  November 13, 2026

Yemen’s Houthis said on Thursday that their forces targeted Saudi Arabia’s East-West pipeline and an oil facility in the Red Sea port city of Yanbu.

Spokesperson Yahya Saree claimed both attacks — and two others on a Saudi airport and an airbase near the Yemeni border — had been successful.
