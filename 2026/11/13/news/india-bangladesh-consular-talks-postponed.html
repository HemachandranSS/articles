India-Bangladesh consular talks postponed over rift

Kallol Bhattacherjee

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

The fifth India-Bangladesh consular dialogue, scheduled for November 17 and 18 in Dhaka, has been postponed amid growing tensions over Sheikh Hasina’s presence in India and her announced plan to return next month.

On November 9, a London-based legal firm representing Ms. Hasina sought a new passport and national ID card from the High Commission of Bangladesh in New Delhi. Acting Foreign Minister Humayun Kabir said Dhaka would not issue a passport and would instead seek extradition under an “acceptable prisoner exchange process.”

The Director-General-level talks were to cover border management, visas and repatriation of detained citizens — the first such meeting in over two years after New Delhi on May 29, 2024. Indian visas for Bangladeshis fell sharply in August 2024 but have risen gradually since Tarique Rahman’s election in February 2026.
