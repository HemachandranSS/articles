PMO has sanctioned prosecution of two IAS officers, HC told

Mohamed Imranullah S.

CHENNAI  |  November 13, 2026

The Prime Minister’s Office has granted sanction to prosecute IAS officers K.S. Kandasamy and K. Vijaya Karthikeyan under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, Additional Solicitor General A.R.L. Sundaresan told the Madras High Court.

The proceedings relate to a ₹98.25-crore Corporation tender irregularities case registered primarily against former AIADMK Minister S.P. Velumani, covering alleged irregularities between 2014 and 2018 in the Greater Chennai and Coimbatore Corporations.

Jayaram Venkatesan of Arappor Iyakkam filed a contempt petition in 2025 over delays. The DVAC FIR was registered in 2021; then Speaker M. Appavu granted sanction to prosecute Mr. Velumani on February 12, 2024. On September 16, 2026, Joint Secretary (DoPT) Sushil Kumar Patel informed Justice N. Anand Venkatesh that the PMO was the competent authority. The judge remarked on the “long-drawn process” of obtaining sanctions for influential people compared with faster movement for the poor.
