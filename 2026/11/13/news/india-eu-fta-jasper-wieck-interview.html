INTERVIEW · TRADE · GERMANY

‘India-EU FTA can be a game changer’

German Ambassador terms deal a ‘win-win’ situation; he says German firms manufacturing in India can re-export to Europe, India’s auto industry can become internationally more competitive through supply chain integration, and Tamil Nadu and other industrial hubs stand to benefit from the FTA

Narayan Lakshman

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

German Ambassador to India Jasper Wieck says the India-EU Free Trade Agreement can be a game changer — a win-win for manufacturing, auto supply chains and industrial hubs such as Tamil Nadu.

On the FTA and industry

German firms making in India could re-export to Europe; India’s auto sector could gain competitiveness through deeper supply-chain integration. Tamil Nadu remains a prime destination (for example Daimler Trucks at Chennai-Oragadam), with interest also in Hosur and Coimbatore.

On climate and defence

Germany commits roughly €10 billion worldwide for decarbonisation, with India a privileged partner under the Green and Sustainable Development Partnership, including renewables and green hydrogen. On Project 75(I), German shipyard TKMS is ready to build a submarine production hub in India with rising local content and possible third-country exports.

On skills and mobility

Indian students in Germany have grown from about 17,000 to 70,000 in nine years. Germany issues around 60,000 long-term visas a year to Indians and seeks migration-mobility agreements to fill skill gaps.

Key Terminologies

  1. India–EU FTA (இந்தியா–ஐரோப்பிய ஒன்றிய வர்த்தக ஒப்பந்தம்)
  2. Supply chain integration (விநியோகச் சங்கிலி ஒருங்கிணைப்பு)
  3. Project 75(I) (திட்டம் 75(I))
  4. Green hydrogen / GSDP (பசுமை ஹைட்ரஜன்)
