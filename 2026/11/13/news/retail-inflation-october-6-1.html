Retail inflation goes past RBI’s limit to 6.1% in Oct.

Almost all categories in the index hit series highs as inflation crosses the targeted comfort band for the first time in two years; inflation in food and beverages rises to 8.5%, transport sector at 5.8%

T.C.A. Sharad Raghavan

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

Retail inflation rose to 6.1% in October, crossing the Reserve Bank of India’s upper threshold of 6% for the first time in two years. Almost all categories in the Consumer Price Index hit series highs on the new CPI series with a 2024 base year.

The RBI targets 4% inflation with a 2% comfort band (2%–6%). Inflation last crossed 6% in October 2024, when it stood at 6.2%.

Food and fuel prices led the uptick, but pressure has spread across sectors. Inflation in food and beverages rose to 8.5%, the transport sector to 5.8%, and restaurant and accommodation services jumped to 10.1%.

Pranjal Ghate, an economist at Brickwork Ratings, said the pressure was supply-driven but reinforced by strong festive-season demand, which also backed the RBI’s decision to raise the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50%.

Key Terminologies

  1. Retail inflation / CPI (சில்லறை பணவீக்கம்)
  2. Comfort band (2%–6%) (இலக்கு வரம்பு)
  3. Repo rate hike (ரெப்போ உயர்வு)
  4. Food & beverages inflation (உணவு பணவீக்கம்)
