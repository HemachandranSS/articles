TNPDCL seeks permission to procure 3,000 MW electricity

R. Srikanth

CHENNAI  |  November 13, 2026

The TNPDCL has increased its proposed power procurement quantum from 1,500 megawatts (MW) to 3,000 MW via a medium-term tender agreement.

The original proposal for 1,500 MW was submitted to the TNERC in February this year. A senior TNPDCL official said the decision to double the quantum reflects power demand of about 19,000 MW and an extended summer.

The proposal is to buy 3,000 MW of round-the-clock (RTC) power for five years under Finance, Operate and Own guidelines.

Key Terminologies

  1. Medium-term tender (இடைக்கால டெண்டர்)
  2. RTC power (முழுநேர மின்சாரம்)
  3. TNERC approval (டிஎன்இஆர்சி அனுமதி)
  4. Finance, Operate and Own (நிதி-இயக்கம்-உரிமை)
