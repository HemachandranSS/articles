New infra projects sanctioned along border with China

Vijaita Singh

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

Amid continuing face-offs with the Chinese Army along the Line of Actual Control (LAC), the Indo-Tibetan Border Police has moved on new border infrastructure in Arunachal Pradesh.

In February, the Cabinet Committee on Security cleared the third phase of the Indo-China Border Roads (ICBR-III) project — 26 roads along the LAC in Arunachal Pradesh. The Union government has not yet officially announced ICBR-III. Phase I (2005) covered 27 priority roads (608 km); Phase II (September 21, 2020) covered 32 roads (683 km) after the Galwan clashes.

Since September, the ITBP has authorised 15 pedestrian suspension bridges and 18 log bridges in Upper Subansiri and Kurung Kumey. Former ITBP Director-General Shatrujeet Kapur said on October 22 that face-offs continue at several locations in Arunachal and praised Shaurya commandos’ role.
