First chargesheet filed in Veeramani case, govt. tells HC

It was filed before special court for POCSO Act cases; probe team intends to file multiple chargesheets in the coming days, says State government

Mohamed Imranullah S.

CHENNAI  |  November 13, 2026

A special investigation team has filed the first chargesheet against R. Veeramani, founder of Gem Granites, in serial child sexual abuse cases, State Public Prosecutor R. John Sathyan told the Madras High Court before Justice V. Lakshminarayanan.

The chargesheet was filed before a special court for the POCSO Act, 2012. The probe team intends to file multiple chargesheets in the coming days.

Justice Lakshminarayanan expressed satisfaction with progress and adjourned connected writ petitions to November 30. The court declined to transfer the case to the CBI, saying the SIT should be allowed to work “in peace” while filing periodic status reports. The SIT is also probing whether any police officials or others tried to protect Veeramani, and is avoiding media briefings to protect sensitive victim information.
