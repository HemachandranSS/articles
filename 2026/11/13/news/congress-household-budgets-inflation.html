Household budgets under strain due to rising costs of essential goods: Congress

The Hindu Bureau

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

The Congress on Thursday targeted the Modi government over rising prices of essential commodities, saying household budgets were under strain and people wanted relief from inflation rather than assurances.

On X, general secretary Jairam Ramesh said prices of milk, rice, flour, pulses, cooking oil, onions, potatoes, tomatoes, sugar and ghee had risen sharply, pointing also to Amul and Mother Dairy milk hikes. “Prime Minister, you once said ‘sab changa si’; now it is ‘sab mehnga si’,” he said.

Citing detergent and home-care price rises of up to 11% since Deepavali, he questioned why sugar and pulses prices had not eased despite higher production and stocks. He also raised a “double burden” of price and quality concerns over the E20 petrol blend.

The people of this country do not want government claims and policy promises; our demand is that inflation be effectively reined in — Jairam Ramesh
