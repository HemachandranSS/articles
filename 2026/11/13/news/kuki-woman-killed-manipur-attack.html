Kuki woman killed, two hurt in Manipur attack

The Hindu Bureau

GUWAHATI  |  November 13, 2026

Unidentified armed men attacked Kuki farmers near Taphou Kuki village on the boundary between Kangpokpi and Senapati districts around 11 a.m. on Thursday, killing 41-year-old Kimneineng Chongloi and injuring David Chongloi and Lamchon Chongloi while they harvested paddy.

The Kangpokpi-based Committee on Tribal Unity (CoTU) said the attack came amid protests over unmet demands after the abduction and killing of Paoginlal Hangshing and Tinginlun Chongloi on October 28. The State government has handed the case to the NIA.

CoTU criticised the State and Central governments for indifference and selective response, warning they would be held responsible if civilian security failed.
