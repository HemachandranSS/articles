Metrowater prepares plan to expand services across Chennai Metropolitan Area

A feasibility report has been prepared mapping the existing assets related to water and sewer facilities and assessing the infra gaps; it has also examined the availability of water sources

K. Lakshmi

CHENNAI  |  November 13, 2026

Chennai Metrowater is preparing to expand water supply and sewerage services across the entire Chennai Metropolitan Area (CMA), based on a feasibility report by consultant Shah Technical Consultants linked to a 2023-24 Budget announcement.

Metrowater now supplies about 1,143 million litres a day (mld) within Greater Chennai Corporation limits (population served about 81.60 lakh; area 426 sq. km). CMA demand is projected to rise to 2,212 mld by 2060 for a population of about 1.24 crore across 1,189 sq. km.

Proposed expansion covers local bodies including Tambaram, Avadi, Poonamallee, St. Thomas Mount, Thirumazhisai, Kundrathur, Minjur and Kattankulathur. New sources under study include the Madurantakam Tank and 173 additional sources identified by the Water Resources Department that could yield nearly 1,596 mld. The report is to be submitted to the State government for approval.
