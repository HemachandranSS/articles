Two missing special police officers found dead in J&K

J&K Police have not issued any formal statement on the incident, nor confirmed involvement of terrorists; security forces intensified anti-terror operations in Chenab Valley, especially in Kishtwar

Peerzada Ashiq

SRINAGAR  |  November 13, 2026

Two special police officers (SPOs) of the J&K Police were found dead with bullet wounds after going missing from a dense forest in the upper reaches of the Chenab Valley.

They were identified as Rashpal Kumar (45) and Bobby Chand (57), both residents of Kiyar Dachhan, Kishtwar. They went missing while accompanying a team of the Army’s 17 Rashtriya Rifles to the Basnan area and were part of temporary operating bases set up after increased terrorist activity since 2020.

Security forces intensified anti-terror operations in Chenab Valley and Kishtwar. A reward of ₹30 lakh has been announced for information on suspected terrorists Muhammad Amin (alias Jehangir), Riaz Ahmad (alias Hazari) and Mudassir Hussain. Separately, terrorists opened fire on the CRPF in south Kashmir’s Kulgam, injuring three personnel.

Chief Minister Omar Abdullah expressed grief and condemned the violence. NC president Farooq Abdullah urged Pakistan to end support for terrorism and called for revival of SAARC and dialogue. BJP legislator Shagun Parihar described it as a tragic and saddening incident.
