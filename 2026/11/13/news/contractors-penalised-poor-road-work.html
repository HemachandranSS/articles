Contractors to be penalised for poor road work

Contractors will be asked to redo the road works after the first violation, followed by a warning for repeat violation; contract to be terminated at the third instance of non-compliance; GCC to take up the second phase of works to repair around 5,000 potholes as the first phase nears completion

R. Aishwarya

CHENNAI  |  November 13, 2026

The Greater Chennai Corporation has issued a circular introducing penalties for defective road work. Contractors will redo work after the first violation, receive a warning for a repeat violation, and face contract termination and blacklisting at the third instance of non-compliance.

Earlier there were no specific penalty clauses—only advisories. Phase one of pothole repairs, allotted ₹22.5 crore for about 7,000 potholes, is nearly complete. Phase two will cover around 5,000 more potholes identified after recent rains.

The civic body has floated tenders worth ₹86.5 crore for relaying and improving roads in Madhavaram, Thiru-Vi-Ka Nagar, Royapuram, Ambattur, Anna Nagar, Teynampet, Kodambakkam, Valasaravakkam, Alandur, Adyar, Perungudi and Sholinganallur. Works include milling and relaying bituminous layers.

Residents and drivers have complained of bumpy stretches and skidding risk from cement patches. Pink autorickshaw driver Deepa in Egmore and motorist Murugan in Teynampet described the strain of poorly finished roads.
