Govt. mulls Special Session to pass Bill on delimitation

Centre is yet to disclose the dates or duration of proposed Parliament session; it is confident of having ‘at least 15-16 more votes than needed’ to pass Bill; move will not work, says Congress leader

The Hindu Bureau

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

The Centre is considering a Special Session of Parliament to pass the Delimitation Bill, 2026, linked to operationalising the Women’s Reservation Act, 2023.

A two-thirds majority needs 361 votes in the 543-seat Lok Sabha (three vacancies). The amendment seeks to raise Lok Sabha strength from 543 to a maximum of 850 to enable a 33% women’s quota based on the 2011 Census. The Monsoon Session was adjourned sine die on August 13 but not prorogued for 91 days — longer than the 2008 record of 87 — allowing a short-notice sitting.

Congress leader Jairam Ramesh called Amit Shah’s push a “delimitation plantation” and said the bulldozer would not work again.

Key Terminologies

  1. Special Session (சிறப்பு அமர்வு)
  2. Delimitation / 850 seats (தொகுதி மறுவரையறை)
  3. Two-thirds majority (361) (மூன்றில் இரண்டு பெரும்பான்மை)
  4. Women’s Reservation Act (பெண்கள் இடஒதுக்கீடு சட்டம்)
