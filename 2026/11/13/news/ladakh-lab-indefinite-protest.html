Ladakh civil society group begins indefinite protest

Peerzada Ashiq

SRINAGAR  |  November 13, 2026

The Leh Apex Body (LAB) began an indefinite sit-in at Nawang Dorjay Stobdan Memorial Park in Leh on Thursday over the MHA’s failure to resume talks and the decision to hold hill council elections in peak winter.

Demands include opposition to simultaneous elections; convening the MHA sub-committee; release of the judicial enquiry report on a September 24 incident; unconditional withdrawal of cases; an independent delimitation commission; and rollback of the new liquor policy.

Climate activist Sonam Wangchuk was shifted to his Leh residence under Section 163(1) of the BNSS; he called the curbs bad in law, arbitrary, vague and disproportionate. LAB co-chairman Tsering Dorjey Lakrook said already 25% of the population had left Ladakh and winter was not the right time for elections.
