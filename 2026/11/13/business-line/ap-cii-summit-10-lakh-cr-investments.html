AP eyes ₹10-lakh-cr investments at CII summit; CM touts high conversion rate

G Naga Sridhar

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Andhra Pradesh expects over 300 MoUs worth about ₹9.89 lakh crore at the 31st CII Partnership Summit. On Thursday alone the State inked MoUs worth ₹5.84 lakh crore with potential for 1,07,693 jobs.

CM N. Chandrababu Naidu said investments since June 2024 total ₹32 lakh crore with a 74% conversion rate. Kia pledged $2 billion; ReNew ₹80,000 crore; CleanMax ₹50,000 crore; Shree Cement ₹7,000 crore. Shipping Minister Sarbananda Sonowal called AP India’s Eastern Maritime Gateway.

Key Terminologies

  1. MoU conversion rate (ஒப்பந்த மாற்று விகிதம்)
  2. CII Partnership Summit (சிஐஐ உச்சிமாநாடு)
  3. Eastern Maritime Gateway (கிழக்கு கடல் வாயில்)
  4. Investment protection law (முதலீட்டு பாதுகாப்பு சட்டம்)
