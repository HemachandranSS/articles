Mittal eyes global deals in telecom to drive growth

HIS CALL: Bharti chief says next phase hinges on satcom, AI infra, and financial services

S Ronendra Singh, Poornima Joshi & Thomas K Thomas

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Sunil Bharti Mittal said Bharti Enterprises is renewing its global expansion drive, eyeing overseas telecom acquisitions backed by improved cash flows and lower debt.

“There is a very small cohort of Indian companies with the potential to go global. And, I think we are one of those.”

Africa operations (from the 2010 Zain buy) have stabilised after restructuring. Over the next five years the group will focus on satcom, data centres, AI compute, sovereign cloud and financial services. Bharti is working with operators including Starlink and OneWeb rather than building its own constellation. Data-centre capacity is targeted at about 1 GW by 2030, with projects in Visakhapatnam, Mumbai, Kolkata and Hyderabad, plus GPU leasing and a 49% stake in Haier’s India appliances business.
