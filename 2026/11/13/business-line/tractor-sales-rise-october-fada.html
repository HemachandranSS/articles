Tractor sales rise 3.1% in October on steady rural demand

T E Raja Simhan

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Tractor retail sales rose 3.1% in October to 79,285 units from 76,898 a year earlier, FADA data showed. The top five makers held 76.2% of the market.

Mahindra & Mahindra sold 18,263 units (+7.6%). Steady rural demand and non-farm use helped; Crisil flagged monsoon-related risks for rabi purchasing power.
