Data centre economy: AI infra push in Q2, a boost for companies

Rohan Das (with inputs from Sindhu Hariharan)

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

India’s data-centre and AI infrastructure build-out lifted revenues for suppliers of compute, cables, power systems and liquid cooling in Q2 FY27.

Redington’s Technology Solutions Group grew 91%. Netweb’s revenue and PAT rose more than 4x; AI business grew 1,068%. Industry capacity is about 1.5 GW, with plans toward ~10 GW by decade-end.
