In major reboot, AI’s poster boy Huang ousted as Nvidia CEO

Bloomberg

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Jensen Huang has been ousted as Nvidia CEO after reports of two affairs, including one with a woman suspected of spying for China. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO.

Huang, 63, co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and had been its only CEO; the firm lacked a clear succession plan. Shares had soared more than 7,000 times since the 1999 IPO, with valuation topping $5 trillion. He had backed OpenAI, Anthropic and cloud firms such as CoreWeave; critics including Michael Burry warned circular deals were inflating AI demand.
