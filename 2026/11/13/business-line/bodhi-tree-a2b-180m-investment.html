James Murdoch’s Bodhi Tree Systems invests $180 m in Adyar Ananda Bhavan

Our Bureau

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Bodhi Tree Systems is investing $180 million for a majority stake in Adyar Ananda Bhavan (A2B). Uday Shankar will chair the new board.

Plans include digital capabilities, modernising kitchens, expanding healthy foods and preparing for an IPO. It is Bodhi Tree’s third major India investment after Allen Career Institute and Jio-Star.
