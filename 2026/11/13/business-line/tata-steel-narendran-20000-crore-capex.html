We are aiming at ₹20,000 crore capex this year: Tata Steel CEO

Suresh P Iyengar

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Tata Steel MD & CEO T.V. Narendran said the company is targeting ₹20,000 crore capex this year (₹15,000 crore in India), after spending ₹6,800 crore in H1.

Net debt increased during Q2 to ₹90,000-91,000 crore… We expect the working capital to normalise in Q3.

Q2 costs rose about ₹1,400 crore from the Gulf conflict. The UK EAF project is due March 2028, with full grid power delayed to March 2029. Annual cost-savings target: ₹7,000 crore.

Key Terminologies

  1. ₹20,000 cr capex (ரூ.20,000 கோடி மூலதனச் செலவு)
  2. Net debt / working capital (நிகரக் கடன் / சுழல் மூலதனம்)
  3. UK EAF project (யுகே மின் உலைத் திட்டம்)
  4. Coking coal costs (கோக்கிங் நிலக்கரி செலவு)
