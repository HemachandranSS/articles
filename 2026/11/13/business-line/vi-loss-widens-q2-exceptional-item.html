Vi’s loss widens to ₹5,975 cr in Q2 on exceptional item

MIXED BAG: 4G/5G subscribers rise to 131 million; bank debt at ₹943 crore

Vallari Sanzgiri

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Vodafone Idea reported a net loss of ₹5,975 crore for the September quarter, up from ₹5,524 crore a year ago, hit by an adverse mark-to-market impact on treasury assets. Adjusted loss was ₹5,513 crore.

Revenue rose 6.2% y-o-y to ₹11,884 crore; EBITDA ₹4,981 crore. ARPU reached ₹198; 4G/5G base 131 million. Bank debt stood at ₹943 crore with cash of ₹5,470 crore.

Key Terminologies

  1. Exceptional MTM item (விதிவிலக்கான எம்டிஎம் விளைவு)
  2. ARPU (பயனர் சராசரி வருவாய்)
  3. 4G/5G subscriber base (4ஜி/5ஜி சந்தாதாரர்கள்)
  4. Bank debt / capex orders (வங்கிக் கடன் / மூலதனச் செலவு)
