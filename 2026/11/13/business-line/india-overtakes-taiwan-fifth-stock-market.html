India overtakes Taiwan to reclaim 5th spot among top stock markets

GREEN SHOOTS: Foreign inflows, stronger earnings, and lower crude oil prices

Our Bureau

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

India regained the world’s fifth-largest stock market spot with listed equities valued around $5.4–5.5 trillion, overtaking Taiwan. The Nifty 50 closed at 25,255.05 and the Sensex at 81,879.40.

FPIs bought a net ₹65,735 crore of Indian equities in November (through Nov 11), while DIIs sold ₹40,504 crore.
