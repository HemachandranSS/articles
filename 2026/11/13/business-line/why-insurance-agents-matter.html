Why insurance agents matter

Digital insurance will grow, but universal coverage depends on human agency. Commission reform must recognise this fact

Casparus Kromhout

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Toward “Insurance for All by 2047,” Shriram Life MD & CEO Casparus Kromhout argues that life insurance is explained and sold, not merely bought — so agency networks remain essential in Tier III and rural India.

The future of insurance coverage distribution is unlikely to be purely physical or purely digital. It will be phygital.

FY24–25 channel data show agency still dominates policies (≈74%) and nearly half of premium, while online remains under 2% of premium. Commission reform should reward persistency, suitability and servicing — not just first-sale volumes — as the winning model pairs agents with technology.
