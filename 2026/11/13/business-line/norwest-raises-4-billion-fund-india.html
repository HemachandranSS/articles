Norwest raises $4 billion fund, lines up 6-8 investments in India

Suresh P Iyengar

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Global investor Norwest closed its largest fund, Norwest 18, at $4 billion, lifting AUM to $18 billion. India remains a core market with the US and Israel.

MD Niren Shah said 6–8 India deals are under evaluation. The firm deploys $250–300 million a year in India, with 41 portfolio firms and ~$2.5 billion India AUM, and over 30 exits including 10 IPOs.
