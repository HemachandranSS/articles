Advantage India

Trump’s poll debacle could help India in trade talks

Editorial Desk

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

Republicans’ mid-term loss of both Houses complicates U.S.–India trade talks. Trump may keep a trade offensive, but Democratic Senators will drive a harder bargain — including via the proposed Lindsey O Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act, 2026 with 100% tariffs on top buyers of Russian oil.

Flashpoints include Section 232 steel/aluminium duties, Section 301 probes, a 10% tariff linked to forced-labour findings, and the so-called “great transshipment scam” investigations. After the Supreme Court struck down reciprocal tariffs (Feb 20), duties were negotiated down from 25% to 18%. With Trade Promotion Authority lapsed since 2021, India should hold firm on agriculture, digital access and IP while seeking market access without huge sacrifices.

Key Terminologies

  1. Section 232 / 301 duties (பிரிவு 232 / 301 வரிகள்)
  2. Russian oil buyer tariffs (ரஷ்ய எண்ணெய் வாங்குநர் வரிகள்)
  3. Trade Promotion Authority (வர்த்தக விரைவு அதிகாரம்)
  4. Transshipment / forced labour tariffs (மாற்று ஏற்றுமதி / கட்டாய உழைப்பு வரி)
