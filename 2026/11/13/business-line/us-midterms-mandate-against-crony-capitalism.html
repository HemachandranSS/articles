US mid-terms, a mandate against crony capitalism

When wealth inequality reaches extreme levels, it often triggers a structural shift in political, social, and economic policy

Jamal Mecklai

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

The U.S. mid-term results, argues Jamal Mecklai, amount to a rejection of crony capitalism and extreme wealth inequality associated with the Trump era — including the political setback for figures such as Elon Musk after heavy campaign spending.

The piece links the vote to demands for fairer taxation (citing New York’s Zohran Mamdani as a signal), risk of an AI / “Magnificent 7” funding backlash, shifts in Israel’s politics away from Netanyahu, and a broader global turn toward permanent fiscal interventions and more humanistic, less purely globalised politics.
