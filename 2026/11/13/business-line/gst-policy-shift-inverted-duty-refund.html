GST policy shift

Services must be included in inverted duty refund

Aditya Nadkarni

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 13, 2026

An inverted duty structure under GST leaves businesses with stranded credit when input rates exceed output rates. Section 54(3) allows refunds, but Rule 89(5) long excluded input services — a gap the Supreme Court flagged in VKC Footsteps while urging GST Council review.

The 57th GST Council backed amending Rule 89(5) to refund ITC on input services and capital goods. Notification 5/2026-Central Tax (Oct 26) applies to services ITC from Nov 1, 2026 and capital goods from April 1, 2027 (60-month spread). Aligning Section 54(3) in the next Budget would lock in the statutory base for neutrality and liquidity.

Key Terminologies

  1. Inverted duty / ITC refund (தலைகீழ் வரி / ஐடிசி பணத்திருப்பம்)
  2. Rule 89(5) / Section 54(3) (விதி 89(5) / பிரிவு 54(3))
  3. Input services & capital goods (உள்ளீட்டு சேவைகள் & மூலதனப் பொருட்கள்)
  4. GST Council / VKC Footsteps (ஜிஎஸ்டி கவுன்சில் / விகேசி தீர்ப்பு)
