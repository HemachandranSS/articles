Centre receives 22 bids for ACC battery manufacturing capacity

Jagriti Chandra

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

The Centre has received 22 bids for 10 GWh of capacity to set up giga-scale Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) battery manufacturing facilities.

RIL, Vikram Solar, Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies and Exide Energy Solutions are among the bidders. The 10 GWh is earmarked for Grid-Scale Stationary Storage Systems. It forms part of the National Programme on ACC Battery Storage targeting 50 GWh, approved in May 2021 with a PLI outlay of ₹18,100 crore. The Ministry of Heavy Industries said strong participation reflects interest in a self-reliant ACC ecosystem.
