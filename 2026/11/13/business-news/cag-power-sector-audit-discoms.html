CAG widens purview of power sector audit, starts with discoms

Saptaparno Ghosh

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

The CAG is undertaking a comprehensive audit of the power sector, beginning with distribution companies (discoms), focusing on consumer satisfaction, power trading and financial sustainability.

The exercise will later cover transmission, generation, renewable energy and the Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS), assessing operational and financial performance over the preceding five years. The ongoing discom phase covers FY21–FY26 for 39 discoms and five departmental undertakings. Privately owned discoms are out of scope. The audit began in July; the report is expected around mid next financial year. Transmission audits are scheduled to begin with FY27.
