‘India will remain a trusted partner for global investors’

Amid global uncertainties, India is doing all it can to ensure safety of the investors’ money, even as wars rage in other parts of the world, says Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal

V. Raghavendra

VISAKHAPATNAM  |  November 13, 2026

At the 31st CII Partnership Summit, Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal said India would remain a trusted partner and reliable destination for global investors amid wars and uncertainty.

He said India was doing all it could to ensure the safety of investors’ money pumped into projects, despite multiple crises and intense competition. Stable policies and governance underpinned that assurance.

India would fully respect and protect intellectual property rights, he said, adding that “India would never steal technology.” He promised support for high-quality manufacturing at competitive cost and highlighted skilled youth contributing to GDP growth.
