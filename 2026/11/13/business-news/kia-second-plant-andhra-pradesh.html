Kia, suppliers plan $2 bn investment in second plant in A.P.

Reuters

NEW DELHI  |  November 13, 2026

South Korea’s Kia and its suppliers plan to invest about $2 billion in a second manufacturing plant in Andhra Pradesh, with production expected in early 2030, after signing an MoU with the State government.

The project is expected to create about 12,000 direct and indirect jobs. MD and CEO Gwanggu Lee said India is central to Kia’s long-term growth. The Anantapur facility will build ICE and electric models, adding 2 lakh vehicles to the annual capacity of 4,30,000 units.
