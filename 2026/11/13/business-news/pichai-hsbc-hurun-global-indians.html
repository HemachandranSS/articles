Pichai tops global Indian leaders’ list as Alphabet hits $4.2 trillion

The Hindu Bureau

BENGALURU  |  November 13, 2026

Sundar Pichai tops the HSBC Hurun Global Indians List 2026 as Alphabet’s valuation reached $4.2 trillion — a 98% surge that overtook Microsoft.

Satya Nadella (Microsoft, $2.6 trillion) ranks second; Sanjay Mehrotra (Micron, $1.4 trillion) third. Prominent newcomers include Shailesh Jejurikar (P&G, 6th) and Kunal Shah (WhatsApp, 14th). Jayshree V. Ullal (Arista, $208 billion) leads women on the list.

Companies led by global Indians are worth $15 trillion (up 53% since 2024); the top five account for $9.1 trillion. The 2026 edition lists 236 individuals across 200 companies (89 newcomers, 16 women), employing 5.3 million people and generating $2.5 trillion in revenue. Entry threshold rose to $2.5 billion.
