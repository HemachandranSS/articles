Iran ready to reopen Hormuz if U.S. agrees to demands: Baqaei

Agence France-Presse

TEHRAN  |  November 3, 2026

Iran offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if Washington meets Tehran’s conditions, Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baqaei said — describing the move as good faith after months of closure that sent energy prices soaring.

The energy corridor has been effectively blockaded since fighting escalated in February. President Donald Trump’s midterm election rallies featured threats to give Iran “the hammer,” sharpening subsequent diplomacy.

Baqaei said messages move through mediators. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi cited “intensive consultations and deliberations” given the gravity of the situation.
