Israel seeks to minimise Palestine population: UN panel chair

Stanly Johny

November 3, 2026

Justice Srinivasan Muralidhar, chair of the UN’s Independent International Commission of Inquiry, said Israel’s policy aims to maximise occupation while minimising the Palestinian population through systematic strategies.

Deliberate attacks

He cited evidence of genocidal crimes in Gaza — including targeting children and destroying life-sustaining infrastructure — alongside historical displacement from 1947–49 and Israeli control of roughly 70% of Gaza’s territory today. Reports describe forces killing unarmed Palestinians, including an eight-month pregnant woman.

Justice Muralidhar discussed universal jurisdiction to prosecute crimes against humanity, scepticism about a viable two-state arrangement, and forcible displacement as policy tools. He noted India is among countries supplying small arms to Israel.
