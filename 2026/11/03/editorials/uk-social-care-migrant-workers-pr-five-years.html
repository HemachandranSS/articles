Many U.K. social care migrant workers to get PR in five years

Sriram Lakshman

LONDON  |  November 3, 2026

Britain softened its earned settlement scheme: foreign social and home care workers who arrive before May 2025 may secure PR in five years instead of a previously floated fifteen — a U-turn after public consultation and union pressure.

Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood’s shift affects many Indian workers, including large Kerala-origin cohorts in care homes. Visa holders must meet salary thresholds in the qualifying period — e.g. about £25,000/year for social care to settle in five years; higher bands for doctors and nurses at £50,271 (five years) or £125,140 (three years).

Net migration fell from a record 944,000 (March 2023) to 171,000 (December 2025) amid tighter rules. Shadow Home Secretary Chris Philp cited a £10 billion cost for the reversal in the House of Commons. Campaigners warned of exploitative employers charging illegal fees — a risk settlement reforms must police.
