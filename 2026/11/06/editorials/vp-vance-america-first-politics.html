V-P Vance eyes own trail in ‘America First’ politics

Varghese K. George

November 6, 2026

Vice-President J.D. Vance is carving a separate lane in America First politics after Republican losses in midterm elections, arguing the administration—not voters—abandoned the creed through war on Iran and deference to Israel.

Core reasons

Vance holds pole position for 2028 by critiquing Donald Trump without open rupture—positioned as insider and outsider. He framed the Iran war as political expediency, not genuine America First strategy.

His intellectual formation blends conventional GOP hawkishness with populist disavowal of endless wars. On immigration he still echoes Trump’s hard line while signalling distance on amnesty debates. Analysts see Vance testing whether he can be sole arbiter of a post-Trump MAGA coalition as Secretary of State Marco Rubio and others compete for foreign-policy narrative control.

  3. Israel Deference Debate (இஸ்ரேல் கொள்கை விவாதம்)
  4. MAGA Succession Politics (இயக்கத் தலைமை போட்டி)
