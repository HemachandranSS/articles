Saudi Arabia issues alerts, warns of ‘danger’ in Yanbu

Reuters

RIYADH  |  November 6, 2026

Saudi Arabia warned of potential danger in Yanbu as Yemen’s Houthis kept attacking the kingdom—opening another front in the wider U.S.–Israeli confrontation with Iran.

The Saudi-led coalition said it destroyed a ballistic missile aimed at Medina; spokesperson Turki al-Malki said air defence intercepted the launch on X. Houthis called targeting Medina a “blatant and grave falsehood,” insisting strikes hit only oil facilities and military bases.

Kingdom civil defence also warned Sharurah near the Yemen border. Houthis said they attacked Riyadh’s international airport during Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa’s visit—testing Saudi sovereignty as the world’s top oil exporter and key U.S. ally sought renewed help against the Yemeni militia.
