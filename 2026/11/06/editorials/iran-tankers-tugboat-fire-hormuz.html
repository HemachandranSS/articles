Iran says two tankers and tugboat caught fire in Hormuz Strait

Fars news agency says IRGC Navy ‘took control’ of two LUCAS drones, a U.S.-developed model

Agence France-Presse

TEHRAN  |  November 6, 2026

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards reported separate fires in the Strait of Hormuz: a large tanker hit a mine in an illegal southern passageway; a supertanker suffered a massive blaze exiting the eastern strait, and a tugboat aiding it also burned.

Official maritime bodies had not confirmed the IRGC version at press time. The British monitor UKMTO logged a different incident: a tanker struck by two unidentified projectiles about 50 nautical miles off Qatar’s north coast—highlighting conflicting threat pictures in crowded shipping lanes.

Fars said IRGC Navy electronic warfare units “took control” of two U.S.-developed LUCAS drones, signalling intensified contest for maritime domain awareness amid energy-market volatility.
