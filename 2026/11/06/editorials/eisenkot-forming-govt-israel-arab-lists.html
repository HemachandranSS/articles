Eisenkot closer to forming govt. in Israel with backing of Arab lists

Joint List bagged 11 seats and Ra’am won 8 seats in election; in October 27 polls, neither Netanyahu nor Eisenkot secured enough votes for majority; once President gives mandate to a lawmaker, the person will have 28 days to form government.

Agence France-Presse

JERUSALEM  |  November 6, 2026

Israel’s largest Arab list said it would back Gadi Eisenkot to form the next government, boosting his odds over Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

President Isaac Herzog held consultations after the October 27 ballot. Netanyahu, a polarising right-winger, and Eisenkot, promising to “restore national unity,” both failed to secure a parliamentary majority.

Traditionally Herzog grants the mandate to the nominee backed by more parties — favouring Eisenkot after the Joint List (11) and Ra’am (8) officially endorsed him as potential kingmakers with an unprecedented 19 Arab seats.

United Torah Judaism withheld a Netanyahu recommendation without embracing Eisenkot — a tactic to punish Likud and signal votes cannot be taken for granted. Protesters in Jerusalem meanwhile called for a Netanyahu–Eisenkot unity government, underscoring how coalition arithmetic now hinges on Arab parties once outside mainstream legislatures bargaining.
