Nithya Raman elected LA Mayor ousting Karen Bass

Agence France-Presse

LOS ANGELES  |  November 7, 2026

Democratic socialist Nithya Raman ousted incumbent Democrat Karen Bass to lead America’s second-largest city, ending a heated race between one-time allies frustrated with the status quo.

Ms. Bass conceded Thursday when Ms. Raman led 52%–48% with three-quarters counted. India-born Raman, 45, campaigned on “pothole politics,” blaming her predecessor for broken infrastructure while mobilising younger voters angry over housing affordability.

The win adds another city for Democratic Socialists of America, whose activist cadre has expanded municipal power—relevant to diaspora visibility and U.S. urban foreign-policy constituencies on climate and migration.
