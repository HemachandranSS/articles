‘A lot of work’ still needed to reach Ukraine peace deal: EU

Agence France-Presse

BRUSSELS  |  November 7, 2026

The EU said Friday that “a lot of work” remains before a Ukraine peace deal, insisting the bloc will decide its own security after Donald Trump touted a “very big” agreement with Moscow covering the war and “all of Europe.”

Comments fuelled fears of a pivot toward the Kremlin as Washington floated rewriting Europe’s security architecture. EU spokesperson Paula Pinho said peace needs willingness from “the one who first started the war,” adding: “It’s obviously up to the European Union to decide on matters which affect its security.”

NATO stressed allies want a “just and sustainable” end ahead of U.S.-mediated Abu Dhabi meetings. Brussels talks with U.S., Ukrainian, European and NATO officials built on Miami and Oslo sessions. European capitals long reject Moscow’s bid to reshape security rules—a push likely to rattle transatlantic trust.
