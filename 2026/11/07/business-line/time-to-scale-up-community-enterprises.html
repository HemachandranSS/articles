WAY AHEAD · MSME & VIKsit BHARAT

Time to scale up community enterprises

This will require a granular framework that maps the assets, skills, infrastructure gaps and market opportunities of each district, village

S. Mahendra Dev, K.K. Tripathy

Economic Advisory Council to the Prime Minister  |  Views personal  |  November 7, 2026

Reaching a $30 trillion economy by 2047 under Viksit Bharat requires moving fragmented producers from necessity-driven micro-work to opportunity-led community businesses that scale, join supply chains and compete at home and abroad.

India needs to transition from necessity-driven micro-entrepreneurs to opportunity-led businesses that scale, integrate into supply chains and compete in domestic and global markets.

Cluster programmes — the MSME Ministry’s Cluster Development Programme and One-District-One-Product — are starting points. What is missing is district- and village-level mapping of assets, skills, infrastructure gaps and markets; technical partnerships with IITs, IIMs and universities; shared advanced manufacturing and testing facilities; ITI curricula tied to long on-site incubation; digital tools for compliance and quality; and market access through ONDC.

India’s 697 geographical indications — mostly handicrafts and agri-products — can anchor rural industrialisation if backed by grants, equity matching, soft loans and collateral-free credit. Khadi and village-industry reforms should modernise technology without abandoning community ownership.
