Low-pressure system over Bay of Bengal likely to become cyclonic storm by Nov. 16

Tamil Nadu will have to wait for a few more days to get greater clarity on the system’s trajectory and its possible influence on the State’s rainfall; Regional Meteorological Centre has forecast light to moderate showers over southern Tamil Nadu and Western Ghats districts till Thursday

The Hindu Bureau

CHENNAI  |  November 14, 2026

A low-pressure area over the southeast Bay of Bengal is expected to move northwest, become a depression on Monday and strengthen into a cyclonic storm over the central Bay by November 16 — possibly named Akvan.

RMC’s V.R. Durai said models do not yet show a TN landfall or major rainfall impact. Light to moderate rain is likely over south Tamil Nadu and the Western Ghats through Nov 19; Chennai may see light rain Saturday.

Key Terminologies

  1. Low pressure / depression (தாழ் அழுத்தம் / தாழ்வுப்பகுதி)
  2. Cyclonic storm Akvan (சூறாவளி அக்வான்)
  3. Northeast monsoon / Bay of Bengal (வடகிழக்கு பருவமழை / வங்காள விரிகுடா)
  4. RMC rainfall forecast (ஆர்எம்சி மழை முன்னறிவிப்பு)
