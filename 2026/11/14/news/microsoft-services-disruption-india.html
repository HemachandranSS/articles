Global disruption of Microsoft services hits Indian businesses

Balasubramanyam C.P.

CHENNAI  |  November 14, 2026

A global disruption of multiple Microsoft services from Thursday into Friday hit Indian firms’ internal communications and customer operations — from start-ups to multinationals.

Microsoft said it was making progress fixing email and workplace-app outages. A major telecom executive said staff could not access mail or send disruption alerts; call-centre loads rose. Atsuya Technologies’ Rahul Ganapathy reported Teams and customer-email trouble. Big Four IT teams in Bengaluru worked to restore services; airlines faced support delays.

Key Terminologies

  1. Microsoft outage (மைக்ரோசாஃப்ட் சேவைத் தடை)
  2. Email / Teams disruption (மின்னஞ்சல் / டீம்ஸ் தடை)
  3. Workplace applications (பணியிடப் பயன்பாடுகள்)
  4. Customer-support delays (வாடிக்கையாளர் ஆதரவு தாமதம்)
