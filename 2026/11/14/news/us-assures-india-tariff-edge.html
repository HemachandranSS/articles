U.S. assures India of tariff edge over its competitors

Both sides working to conclude interim trade deal by fiscal year-end, officials say, with New Delhi insisting on assurances of comparative advantage as agreed in the Trump-Modi joint statement

T.C.A. Sharad Raghavan

NEW DELHI  |  November 14, 2026

The U.S. has assured India a comparative tariff advantage over competitors in an interim trade deal, aligning with the February 2026 Modi–Trump joint statement on reciprocal preferential market access.

Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agrawal said a U.S. team visited India to advance the text, aiming to finish by fiscal year-end. Outstanding issues include a 10% forced-labour tariff and an excess-capacity probe that could bring more duties; India wants the edge to cover those future tariffs too.

Key Terminologies

  1. Interim trade deal (இடைக்கால வர்த்தக ஒப்பந்தம்)
  2. Reciprocal preferential access (பரஸ்பர முன்னுரிமை அணுகல்)
  3. Forced labour tariff / excess capacity (கட்டாய உழைப்பு வரி / அதிக உற்பத்தி திறன்)
  4. Fiscal year-end target (நிதி ஆண்டு இலக்கு)
