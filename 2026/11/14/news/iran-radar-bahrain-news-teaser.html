Iran says it targeted a U.S. radar in Bahrain in retaliatory strike

Stanly Johny

NEW DELHI  |  November 14, 2026

Iran’s IRGC said on Friday it “destroyed” a U.S. early-warning radar in Bahrain in retaliation for a U.S. strike on a telecom tower in Sirik, southern Iran — calling it a warning against “air mischief and oil smuggling” in the Strait of Hormuz.

CENTCOM denied the radar was destroyed and said it intercepted two one-way attack drones launched toward Bahrain.
