Congress slams Modi govt. over soaring cost of living

The Hindu Bureau

NEW DELHI  |  November 14, 2026

Congress communications chief Jairam Ramesh called the situation “mehengaai ka bhuchaal,” saying retail inflation hit a two-year high of 6.09% and food inflation 8.94%, with onions up 116%, garlic 60% and ginger 48%.

From the kitchen to every other daily necessity, prices have spiralled out of control. The Modi government’s misguided policies have broken the backs of ordinary people.

He warned of shrinkflation and possible rate hikes that would raise borrowing costs further.

Key Terminologies

  1. Mehengaai / cost of living (விலைவாசி / வாழ்க்கைச் செலவு)
  2. Retail / food inflation (சில்லறை / உணவுப் பணவீக்கம்)
  3. Shrinkflation (சுருக்கப் பணவீக்கம்)
  4. Interest rate hike risk (வட்டி உயர்வு இடர்)
