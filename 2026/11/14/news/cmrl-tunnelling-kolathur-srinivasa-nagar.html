CMRL completes tunnelling work for 1-km stretch from Kolathur to Srinivasa Nagar

It is a part of Phase 2 project’s Corridor 5 that runs from Madhavaram to Sholinganallur; the TBM faced several challenges, including boring through a steep curve and past open wells, say Metro Rail officials

The Hindu Bureau

CHENNAI  |  November 14, 2026

CMRL finished a 1-km tunnel from Kolathur to Srinivasa Nagar on Corridor 5 (Madhavaram–Sholinganallur) of the 118.9-km Phase 2 project after nearly a year of boring through weathered and hard rock.

The TBM negotiated a 230-m-radius curve, varied geology and about 70 bore/open wells beneath residences. Delays earlier stemmed from high tender quotes and resident opposition.
