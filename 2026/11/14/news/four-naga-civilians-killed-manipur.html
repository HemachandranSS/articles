Four Naga civilians killed in Manipur highway ambush

The assailants ambushed two vehicles and opened fire; Kuki groups deny any involvement; the attack comes a day after a Kuki woman died and two Kuki men got injured in a similar incident

The Hindu Bureau

GUWAHATI  |  November 14, 2026

Unidentified gunmen ambushed a hatchback and a pickup on NH-202 at New Haven, Litan (Ukhrul) around 1 p.m. Friday, killing four Naga civilians on the spot: Kapangkhui R.S., Haoleila R.S., Khanmila Keising and August Ngalung.

Police, BSF, Army and Assam Rifles reinforced the area. Ukhrul is Naga-majority; adjoining Kangpokpi is Kuki-majority. Kuki groups denied involvement and sought an impartial probe. The wider Kuki–Naga conflict has claimed more than 65 lives since February 2026.
