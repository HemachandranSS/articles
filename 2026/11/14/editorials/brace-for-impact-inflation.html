Brace for impact

High inflation due to external factors could soon exacerbate economic pain

Editorial Desk

Editorial  |  November 14, 2026

India’s resilience is being tested as retail inflation crossed the RBI’s 6% upper limit in October 2026, with economists expecting elevated prices through Q3. Food inflation hit about 8.5% amid monsoon shortfalls, and 10 of 12 major CPI categories recorded high readings.

Data for India research shows nominal wages roughly doubled from 2012 to 2024 but fell about 4% in real terms — sharper for higher-skilled workers — threatening consumption and investment. Double-digit growth in cash with the public and UPI transactions, outpacing nominal GDP, may mean official inflation understates the true price squeeze. With limited quick fixes, the path is sector-specific growth and clear public communication.
