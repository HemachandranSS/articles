Pakistan’s Army says it killed 74 militants in raids on their hideouts; car bomb kills 5

Associated Press

ISLAMABAD  |  November 14, 2026

Pakistani forces said they killed 74 militants over 10 days in raids near the Afghan border — 43 in Mir Ali and Orakzai (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa) and 31 in Balochistan, where the outlawed Baloch Liberation Army has led an insurgency since November 3.

The military alleged backing from Afghanistan and India; Kabul and New Delhi have denied such claims. President Asif Ali Zardari and PM Shehbaz Sharif vowed to keep fighting militancy. Separately, a BLA-claimed car bomb at a Kandawa checkpoint in Mastung killed five and injured 13.
