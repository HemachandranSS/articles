U.S. strikes island in Hormuz as Iran says ‘its forces have grown stronger’

Friday’s strikes are the first since Trump’s Republican Party was dealt a stunning rejection in the U.S. midterm elections; Washington says it targeted an air defence system on Qeshm island; Iran Guards lift limit on long-range ballistic missiles.

Associated Press

WASHINGTON  |  November 8, 2026

The U.S. military struck an air defence system on Qeshm island in the Strait of Hormuz—among the first U.S. blows since Republicans faced a stunning rejection in midterm elections.

Iran said its forces have “grown stronger” at the outset of fighting, with the IRGC removing a 2,000-km cap on long-range ballistic missiles. State outlets reported a tanker fire after hitting a naval mine in Hormuz waters. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and Qatar’s Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani discussed diplomacy as Tehran fortified its grip on messaging via Tasnim and Fars agencies.
