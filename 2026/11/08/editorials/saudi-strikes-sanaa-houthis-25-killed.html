25 killed, dozens wounded in Saudi strikes near Yemen’s Sanaa: Houthis

Associated Press

CAIRO  |  November 8, 2026

Air strikes killed at least 25 people—mostly women and children—and left about 30 wounded, the Houthi-run Health Ministry said. In the aftermath, rescuers pulled limp victims from rubble.

Hospital images underscored civilians in dire straits in Houthi-controlled Sanaa. Separately, reports described Houthi efforts to manage Red Sea shipping via email “safe passage” criteria—functioning as a de facto maritime blockade affecting vessels transiting the region under militia criteria, tightening the Saudi–Houthi front tied to wider Iran-war dynamics.
