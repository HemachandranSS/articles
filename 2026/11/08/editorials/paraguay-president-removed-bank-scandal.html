Paraguay President removed from office over bank scandal

Associated Press

ASUNCION  |  November 8, 2026

The Senate ousted President Santiago Pena and placed him under house arrest after the collapse of government-linked Ueno bank. Vice-President Pedro Alliana was sworn in until August 2028.

Lawmakers cited breach of trust and influence peddling, alleging hundreds of millions in public funds were channelled to Ueno while regulators overlooked insider lending. Millions of depositors could not access cash until partial payouts began November 7 for balances up to about $1,600—an impeachment shock with regional diplomatic ripples given Peña’s ties to Trump-aligned politics.
