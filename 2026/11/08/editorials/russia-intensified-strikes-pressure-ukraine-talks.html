Russia has intensified strikes to pressure us during talks: Ukraine

Agence France-Presse

KYIV  |  November 8, 2026

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Moscow has intensified strikes to pressure Kyiv before diplomacy, after an overnight barrage of 172 drones and 36 ballistic missiles hit energy infrastructure.

Blackouts struck Dnipropetrovsk, Donetsk, Kyiv, Zhytomyr and Kharkiv, hampering recovery days before a trilateral U.S.-brokered meeting in Abu Dhabi on November 10. Zelenskyy said the timing was meant to lay the groundwork for Russian demands rather than good-faith ceasefire steps.
