Six people dead after gunman opens fire in Polish city; one held

Agence France-Presse

KRAKOW  |  November 8, 2026

Police said six people were killed and a dozen wounded after a Polish suspect drove onto Krakow’s Main Market Square and shot at random people Saturday before officers wounded and apprehended him.

Four women and two men died; about twelve others were injured. The man wore military fatigues and carried a long gun; officers found more firearms and a large quantity of ammunition in his car, spokeswoman Katarzyna Cislo said. Police did not disclose a motive.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it a tragic shooting, praising officers who risked their lives in the line of duty. President Karol Nawrocki expressed sympathy for casualties—a rare mass-violence event in an EU NATO member amid wider European security anxieties.
