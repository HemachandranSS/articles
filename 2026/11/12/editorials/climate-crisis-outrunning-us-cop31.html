Climate crisis is ‘outrunning us’, UN chief warns COP31

Reuters

ANTALYA  |  November 12, 2026

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres told world leaders at the COP31 summit in Türkiye on Wednesday that the slow international response to climate change had placed the world on a path to destruction, and that fast action is needed to avert it.

His comments set the tone for two days of rapid-fire speeches by world leaders at the UN conference on Türkiye’s Mediterranean coast. The summit opened on Monday.

“The brutal truth is that the climate crisis is outrunning us. This is the path to mutually assured destruction,” Mr. Guterres said, pointing to rising emissions, global temperatures and worsening climate-driven disasters.

“But science is not telling us to surrender. It is telling us to fight harder,” he said. “Fossil fuels must go the way of the dinosaurs.”
