Over 1,000 India jobs at risk in Qualcomm

The Hindu Bureau

CHENNAI  |  November 12, 2026

More than 1,000 jobs could be at risk in India as Qualcomm undertakes a global workforce reduction, although the U.S. chipmaker has not disclosed the number of positions likely to be eliminated in the country.

In an October 19 internal memo to staff, reviewed by Reuters, CEO Cristiano Amon described the exercise as a “performance-focused reduction” following the firm’s annual staff reviews. Notifications to affected staff were likely to begin in November. Mr. Amon said the move was intended to build “a stronger performance culture” and address situations where the firm “has consistently fallen short of expectations”.

Qualcomm did not respond to The Hindu’s queries on whether layoffs had started in India, the number of staff likely to be hit, locations and business divisions involved, or severance terms.

Pan organisation

Pareekh Jain, CEO, EIIRTrend and an IT industry analyst, estimated about 1,000 Indian staff could be affected. He said the cuts appeared to span organisational levels and were not necessarily performance-based. Qualcomm’s annual report put its global workforce at about 57,000 as of September 27. Its 2023 Corporate Responsibility Report showed India accounted for 36% of a global workforce of 50,257 — about 18,100 staff. The firm reported 15,404 staff in India in 2021.

Weakening finances

The workforce reduction comes against a weakening financial backdrop. Qualcomm reported fiscal 2026 revenue of $43.12 billion, down 3% year-on-year. Fourth-quarter revenue fell 8% to $10.32 billion while non-GAAP net income slid 15% to $11.31 billion.

Meanwhile, posts on Reddit’s r/qualcomm community described staff separations in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, including notifications on November 11. Alagunambi Welkin, president, Union of IT and ITES Employees, questioned whether Qualcomm had secured the necessary government approvals for retrenchment and urged staff facing pressure to quit to approach labour authorities.
