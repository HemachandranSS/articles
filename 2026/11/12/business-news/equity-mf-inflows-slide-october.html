Equity mutual fund inflows slide 14% in Oct. from Sept.

Under equity, the larger mid-cap category got ₹9,484 crore; Equity funds stay largest contributor with about 60% of the total folios and 9.64 lakh additions during this particular month

Gowri Lakshmi

MUMBAI  |  November 12, 2026

The Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) recorded net inflows of ₹1.73 lakh crore for the month of October, reversing the outflow seen a month prior. In September, AMFI said there was an outflow of ₹1.33 lakh crore. However, flows into equity mutual funds slid to ₹32,993 crore, down almost 14%, from ₹38,306 crore a month ago.

Under equities, the larger mid-cap category received an inflow of ₹9,484 crore. Equity funds were the largest contributor with about 60% of the total folios and 9.64 lakh additions during the month.

No broad shift

“October’s rebound is best read as a liquidity swing, not a broad shift in investor appetite. The key things to watch are whether liquid fund inflows hold up beyond the quarter-end cycle, and whether equity inflows stay above ₹30,000 crore while FPI selling continues,” Sumit Singhania, head of research at Bajaj Broking, wrote in a note.

Debt funds recorded an inflow of ₹1.13 lakh crore, which took the overall assets under management (AUM) to ₹18.81 lakh crore. Total AUM under hybrid funds reached ₹11.48 lakh crore, with ₹3,514 crore received in October. Passive funds gained ₹23,394 crore with overall AUM at ₹14.79 lakh crore.

Mutual fund folios up

Mutual fund folios rose 0.6% month-on-month to 28.78 crore with 15.80 lakh folios added last month. Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) recorded a monthly contribution of ₹32,160 crore with total SIP assets at ₹17.68 lakh crore. This accounts for 21.25% of the total asset portfolio.

“Despite three months of market volatility, overall growth reached 10%. Looking ahead, if markets stay stable or move upward, as current trends suggest, we expect the growth seen in previous months to continue,” Venkat Chalasani, CEO, AMFI, said.
