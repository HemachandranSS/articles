India raises fertilizer subsidy by 64%, overshoots Budget estimates

Prabhudatta Mishra & Shishir Sinha

NEW DELHI  |  November 12, 2026

The Union Cabinet approved a ₹62,370.02 crore subsidy for different types of fertilizers for the 2026-27 Rabi season, which is ₹24,417.73 crore (64%) more than the ₹37,952 crore budgeted for the 2025-26 Rabi season. With ₹41,533.81 crore subsidy allocated for Kharif 2026, the total outgo on P&K subsidy in FY27 exceeded the ₹1 lakh crore mark against a budgetary allocation of ₹54,000 crore.

As the government had budgeted ₹1.71 lakh crore total fertilizer subsidy including ₹1.17 lakh crore for urea for FY27, the overall outgo may top ₹3 lakh crore as global prices of both urea and LNG (a key manufacturing input) shot up after the Iran war and stayed elevated longer than expected. The Centre had cut fertilizer subsidy by over 8% in FY27 from ₹1.86 lakh crore in FY26, but the Iran war upended expectations of global price easing.

Total spending on fertilizer subsidy in six months till September had crossed 70% of FY27 budget estimates, as per the Controller General of Accounts (CGA). It is estimated total spending for FY27 could touch as high as ₹3.50 lakh crore.

“The higher subsidy bill was expected given global developments on gas. With food inflation rising sharply it is essential to check input costs like fertilizers… We believe fiscal deficit ratio will be higher by up to 0.3% of GDP and this rise has already been factored,” said Madan Sabnavis, chief economist, Bank of Baroda.

(The writers are with The Hindu Business Line)
