Govt. bars export of lithium-ion battery waste

Saptaparno Ghosh

NEW DELHI  |  November 12, 2026

In a positive for India’s mineral recycling industry, the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) barred the export of waste and scrap of primary cells and batteries as well as electric accumulators containing lithium-ion battery black mass. The order would be implemented with immediate effect.

Black mass refers to the particulate powdered mixture containing lithium, nickel, manganese, cobalt and graphite that is recovered during the recycling of lithium-ion batteries. The minerals are particularly essential for India’s electronics and electric vehicles as well as the renewable energy storage ecosystem.

In a statement, the Union Ministry of Commerce and Industry stated the move seeks to ensure that the black mass generated in India is recycled and refined within the country. “The measure is expected to assure feedstock availability for domestic recycling and refining units, improve their capacity utilisation and encourage further investment and employment in the sector,” it stated.
