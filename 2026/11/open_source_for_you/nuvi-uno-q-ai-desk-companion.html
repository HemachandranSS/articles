Nuvi turns UNO Q into AI desk companion

FOSS Bytes

Open Source For You  |  November 2026  |  Page 15, 16

Luca Di Lorenzo’s compact desktop companion robot Nuvi is built on the Arduino UNO Q. Under 30 cm tall, it can detect people, respond to names and hold conversations. The design is inspired by a Shiba Inu named Kaito.

Related coverage notes Gemini for speech understanding and Piper for voice output on the UNO Q board—an approachable path for natural-language control on small open hardware.

Key Terminologies

  1. Nuvi (நுவி)
  2. Arduino UNO Q (ஆர்டுயினோ யூனோ க்யூ)
  3. Piper TTS (பைப்பர் குரல்)
  4. Gemini speech (ஜெமினி பேச்சு)
