Dutch ditch Windows for NixOS

FOSS Bytes

Open Source For You  |  November 2026  |  Page 10

The Dutch government is moving towards digital sovereignty by adopting a fully open-source, NixOS-based desktop environment called DAWO (Digitaal Autonome Werkomgeving Overheid).

It is a modular ecosystem integrating tools such as Nextcloud, Collabora, Element and OpenProject. The project is hosted on Forgejo and mirrored on Codeberg. NixOS was chosen over openSUSE and Fedora for its declarative configuration and reproducible builds, which simplify auditing and maintenance.

  1. DAWO (டிஜிட்டல் தன்னாட்சி பணியிடம்)
