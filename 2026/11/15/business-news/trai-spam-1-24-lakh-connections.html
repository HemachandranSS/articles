TRAI barred 1.24 lakh mobile connections in July-Sept. for spam

Regulator blocked 3,725 SMS headers and blacklisted 374 senders for one year

Press Trust of India

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

Regulator TRAI took enforcement action against more than 1.24 lakh telecom resources in July–September for unsolicited commercial communication violations.

About 1,10,325 resources were barred from outgoing services for 15 days on a first violation, while 13,777 were disconnected for one year for repeat breaches. Another 374 entities were blacklisted for a year and 3,725 SMS headers blocked.

TRAI received 15.29 lakh spam complaints in the quarter, of which 7.23 lakh were classified as actionable.
