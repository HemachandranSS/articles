LPG distributors seek higher commissions and new connections

Industry flags rising costs and falling refill sales as PNG expands; seeks balancing act from Petroleum Minister

Saptaparno Ghosh

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

LPG distributors sought a revision in commissions, permission to issue fresh connections and a balanced approach to PNG expansion during an interaction with Union Petroleum Minister Hardeep Singh Puri.

Executives said refill sales have suffered as PNG gains ground, while commissions last revised in October 2023 have not kept pace with rising technology and safety costs, squeezing operational margins.

They argued consumers should retain freedom to choose between LPG and PNG. The Hindu sought the government’s response.
