Fewer cyclones cross State’s coast during northeast monsoon, weather trends show

IMD data for 1981–2025 show most Bay systems steer north of Tamil Nadu or dissipate at sea

K. Lakshmi

CHENNAI  |  November 15, 2026

Tamil Nadu has seen relatively fewer cyclonic systems crossing its coast during the northeast monsoon in the past 45 years, meteorologists note, even as the Bay churns out its next cyclone.

Of 117 systems that formed over the Bay of Bengal in October–November between 1981 and 2025, only 22 made landfall on the Tamil Nadu coast; 68 moved north towards neighbouring States or countries, and 26 dissipated over the sea without landfall.

IMD climatology suggests that of the three to four systems typically forming between October and December, usually only one approaches or crosses the State — though rainfall can still arrive when systems die over water.
