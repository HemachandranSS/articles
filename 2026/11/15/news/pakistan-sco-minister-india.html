Pakistan to send Minister to India for SCO dialogue

Musadik Masood Malik has been a vocal critic of India’s decision to place Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance after Pahalgam attack; Pak. says committed to promoting dialogue among civilisations

Kallol Bhattacherjee

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

Pakistan’s Climate Change Minister Musadik Masood Malik will attend the SCO Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi from November 20–22 — the first Pakistani ministerial visit since Bilawal Bhutto Zardari’s 2023 Goa trip.

Malik has criticised India’s placing of the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance after the Pahalgam attack. Islamabad nominated him instead of heritage minister Aurangzeb Khan Khichi.

Pakistan said it remains committed to people-to-people contacts within the SCO “in a spirit of mutual respect,” a day after India stressed shared civilisational and cultural past.

Key Terminologies

  1. SCO Civilisational Dialogue (எஸ்சிஓ நாகரிக உரையாடல்)
  2. Indus Waters Treaty abeyance (சிந்து நீர் ஒப்பந்த இடைநிறுத்தம்)
  3. Pahalgam attack (பகல்காம் தாக்குதல்)
  4. People-to-people contact (மக்களிடையே தொடர்பு)
