Nehru is principal architect of modern India: Congress

Leaders pay tribute to India’s first Prime Minister; Kharge recalls Nehru’s commitment to scientific thinking, secularism, and equality, and his faith in providing every citizen with equal opportunities

The Hindu Bureau

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

Calling Jawaharlal Nehru the principal architect of modern India, Congress leaders paid tribute at Shanti Van and the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan on his 137th birth anniversary.

Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge recalled Nehru’s commitment to scientific temper, secularism and equal opportunity. Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, Ashok Gehlot and others joined the homage; TMC’s Derek O’Brien criticised the absence of Union Ministers.

Rahul Gandhi described Nehru as the “Jewel of Hind,” while Jairam Ramesh highlighted digital engagement with the Selected Works of Jawaharlal Nehru archive.

Key Terminologies

  1. Jawaharlal Nehru (ஜவஹர்லால் நேரு)
  2. Scientific temper / secularism (அறிவியல் மனப்பான்மை / மதச்சார்பின்மை)
  3. Shanti Van / Samvidhan Sadan (சாந்தி வன் / சம்விதான் சதன்)
  4. Selected Works archive (தேர்ந்தெடுக்கப்பட்ட படைப்புகள் காப்பகம்)
