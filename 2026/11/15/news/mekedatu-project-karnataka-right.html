Mekedatu project is Karnataka’s right: CM

D.K. Shivakumar says Karnataka will build the balancing reservoir while ensuring Tamil Nadu gets its Supreme Court-stipulated Cauvery share

The Hindu Bureau

BENGALURU  |  November 15, 2026

Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar asserted that Karnataka will go ahead with the Mekedatu balancing reservoir, calling it the State’s right and duty while assuring Tamil Nadu of its stipulated Cauvery share.

Responding to opposition from Tamil Nadu leaders, he invited a delegation to the dam site. Karnataka would respect Tamil Nadu’s entitlement of 177 tmc ft as fixed by the Supreme Court.

“Protecting Karnataka’s water rights while ensuring that Tamil Nadu gets its due share is our responsibility. We will not back down,” he told presspersons.

Key Terminologies

  1. Mekedatu balancing reservoir (மேகேதாட்டு சமநிலை நீர்த்தேக்கம்)
  2. Cauvery water share (காவிரி நீர் பங்கு)
  3. 177 tmc ft entitlement (177 டிஎம்சி அடி உரிமை)
  4. Inter-State water dispute (மாநிலங்களுக்கிடையேயான நீர் தகராறு)
