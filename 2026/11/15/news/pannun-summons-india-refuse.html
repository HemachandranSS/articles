Can India refuse summons in Pannun case?

A U.S. court has allowed Khalistani separatist Gurpatwant Singh Pannun to pursue serving summons on India’s NSA Ajit Doval and former RAW chief Samant Goel through Indian courts

Aaratrika Bhaumik

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

A U.S. court on November 9 permitted Gurpatwant Singh Pannun to pursue serving summons on NSA Ajit Doval and former RAW chief Samant Goel via Indian courts, raising questions under the Hague Service Convention.

India designates the Law and Justice Ministry as central authority under the Hague Service Convention. District Judge Katherine Polk Failla directed transmission to Patiala House, Dwarka and Tis Hazari courts after failed personal service attempts on Doval in the U.S.

Procedural delays are common; MEA spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal dismissed the lawsuit as “fabricated and malicious.” India limits certain diplomatic/consular service channels to nationals of the originating State.

Key Terminologies

  1. Hague Service Convention (ஹேக் சர்வீஸ் மாநாடு)
  2. Central authority / CPC service (மைய ஆணையம் / சிபிசி சம்மன்)
  3. NSA / RAW (தேசிய பாதுகாப்பு ஆலோசகர் / ரா)
  4. Ex-parte civil summons (எதிர் தரப்பின்றி சிவில் சம்மன்)
