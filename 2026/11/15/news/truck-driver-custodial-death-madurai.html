Truck driver detained for questioning dies in custody

R. Balakrishnan of Theni, questioned over a container truck fire, died at Chekkanoorani police station; CB-CID probe ordered

S. Sundar

MADURAI  |  November 15, 2026

Truck driver R. Balakrishnan of Theni, detained for questioning at Chekkanoorani police station over a container truck fire near Valandur, died in custody on Saturday morning.

Police said he went to the restroom around 6 a.m., was found unconscious, and was declared dead at a Madurai hospital. An inspector and two constables were suspended amid public outrage; the case was transferred to the Crime Branch-CID.

The November 3 fire destroyed cardamom bags; Balakrishnan had earlier filed a complaint but was later called to verify CCTV. His wife Sudha said he was questioned late into Thursday night and again on Friday.
