There is more to vanilla than its taste and aroma

Speaking of Science — cultivation, vanillin chemistry and biomedical research around the familiar flavour

D. Balasubramanian

SCIENCE  |  November 15, 2026

Vanilla, brought to India under colonial cultivation and now grown in Kerala, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, is more than a dessert additive: vanillin shows antimicrobial, antioxidant and neuroprotective properties in research reviews.

Scientists have produced vanillin by breaking cell walls of biomass and by genetically modifying microbes. A Mysuru group has described cultivation practices; other teams study blood-pressure and digestive effects.

The column surveys both agricultural heritage and modern biotechnology around the world’s favourite flavour.
