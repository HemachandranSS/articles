Trump may hold off on new strike unless provoked by Iran, Houthis

The U.S. President says he is pursuing a ‘more civil path’ at allies’ request; White House officials say he will decide whether and when to act

Reuters

WASHINGTON/RIYADH  |  November 15, 2026

U.S. President Donald Trump has signalled he may hold off on a new strike unless provoked by Iran or the Houthis, citing requests from regional allies not to return to full-scale war.

He said he could “end it immediately” but is pursuing a “more civil path” at partners’ urging. White House officials stressed that timing and scope remain his call.

Private comments build on public messaging after recent West Asia escalation; Republican losses in Congress were also referenced in related political commentary around the administration’s posture.
