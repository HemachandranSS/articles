Seven arrested for siphoning ₹22.30 crore from T.N. govt. account through cyberfraud

The bank account was maintained by Tamil Nadu Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department and contained funds released by the Union and State governments; the accused fraudulently transferred the amount from the department’s single nodal account between October 12 and 30

R. Sivaraman

CHENNAI  |  November 15, 2026

The Cyber Crime Wing of the Central Crime Branch arrested seven people for siphoning ₹22.30 crore from a single nodal account of the Tamil Nadu Rural Development and Panchayat Raj Department held at HDFC Bank’s CIT Nagar branch.

Funds meant for primary healthcare infrastructure under Fifteenth Finance Commission health grants were moved via unauthorised electronic debits between October 12 and 30 using compromised online-banking credentials, then routed through 24 accounts across Nagapattinam, Ramanathapuram and Tiruppur.

Those arrested include Chellapandian, Karumbur Annadurai, Lokanathan, Abdul Rahman, Jagadish, Ashraf Ali and Kannadasan. Police seized seven phones, 14 SIM cards, a cheque book and a passbook; efforts continue to recover diverted money.
