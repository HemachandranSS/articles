AI rainmaker meets his Waterloo

The Nvidia chief’s abrupt exit marks a stunning fall from grace for the man who built the hardware foundation of the AI revolution

John Xavier

BENGALURU  |  November 15, 2026

Jensen Huang’s abrupt exit marks a stunning fall for the executive who made GPUs and the CUDA platform the hardware foundation of modern AI.

His bet that Nvidia chips could do far more than 3D rendering put the company at the centre of the AI stack. He also argued U.S. chip restrictions only strengthen Chinese makers such as Huawei and that China should stay reliant on the American tech stack — a line that found a receptive audience in President Trump.

The resignation on November 12, after links drawn in an alleged spy scandal, closes a chapter in which Huang expanded Nvidia’s reach across training, inference and data-centre infrastructure.
