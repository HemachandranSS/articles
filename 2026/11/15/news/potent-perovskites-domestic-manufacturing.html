Potent perovskites demand domestic manufacturing push

As China tightens export controls on next-generation photovoltaic technology, local enterprises race to build independent supply chains

Sonam Raina

BENGALURU  |  November 15, 2026

Perovskite solar cells are emerging as a major next wave of photovoltaics, but India’s ability to benefit depends on domestic manufacturing as China tightens export controls on key technology.

National testing facilities and local core equipment are described as vital if lightweight modules are to become commercially viable. Research teams report certified efficiencies above 26% on small-area devices.

The piece argues that without independent supply chains, India risks repeating import dependence seen in earlier solar hardware cycles.
