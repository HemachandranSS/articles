Quad summit likely to be held in Manila on Nov. 17

It is for the first time that the summit will be held in a non-member country; critical minerals supply chains and energy shipping lanes likely on the agenda

Suhasini Haidar

NEW DELHI  |  November 15, 2026

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is set to meet U.S. President Donald Trump, Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi and Australian PM Anthony Albanese at a Quad summit likely on the evening of November 17 in Manila — the first Quad leaders’ meet in a non-member country, on the ASEAN Summit sidelines.

Agenda items expected include critical-minerals supply chains and securing commercial energy shipping lanes, plus seafarer safety in the Strait of Hormuz, Sea of Oman and Black Sea.

India has been unable to host recently amid scheduling constraints; Trump is also due later in Shenzhen for meetings involving Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin.

Key Terminologies

  1. Quad / ASEAN Summit (க்வாட் / ஆசியான் உச்சிமாநாடு)

  3. Energy shipping lanes (ஆற்றல் கப்பல் பாதைகள்)
  4. Indo-Pacific (இந்தோ-பசிபிக்)
