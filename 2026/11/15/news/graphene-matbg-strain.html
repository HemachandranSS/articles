Graphene adds new feather to cap of unusual properties

Strain and stacking in magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene continue to reveal exotic electronic behaviour

The Hindu

SCIENCE  |  November 15, 2026

Researchers continue to uncover unusual electronic properties in graphene systems, including magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene under controlled strain.

Twisting and straining layered carbon sheets can drive correlated states and other exotic phases, expanding graphene’s already remarkable catalogue of behaviours.

The report situates the findings within broader condensed-matter efforts to engineer quantum materials from simple carbon building blocks.
