As air pollution rises, doctors ask people to leave Delhi temporarily

Ankita Upadhyay

NEW DELHI  |  November 11, 2026

With Delhi reeling under severe air pollution, doctors have advised people who have the option to leave the city for a few days until the air quality improves.

Rajesh Chawla, a senior pulmonologist at Apollo Indraprastha Hospital, said the worsening pollution was leading to an increase in patients reporting breathing difficulties, cough and eye irritation.

“Like every year, it will cause us difficulty in breathing, cough, eye irritation and other problems. The number of patients coming to the OPD with these symptoms has increased,” he said.

Advising people who had the option to leave Delhi temporarily, he said they could return once the pollution levels improve. “If it is possible, people should not come to Delhi in the first place. Those who are already staying here can go away for a few days, if they have the option, and return once things settle down,” he said, advising people to wear masks to reduce their exposure.
