Blackstone to acquire 16% stake in IIFL Finance

MONEY BUSINESS. PE giant leads ₹6,414 crore fundraise; NBFC’s promoters co-invest

Our Bureau

MUMBAI  |  November 11, 2026

IIFL Finance Ltd’s Board of Directors on Tuesday approved a primary capital raise of ₹6,414 crore ($686 million) from funds managed by Blackstone Private Equity, the company’s promoters and Anchorage Capital.

The entire investment will raise the NBFC’s capital adequacy ratio from 17 per cent to 30 per cent (proforma September-end 2026), support the next phase of growth across its secured lending businesses, and could improve its credit ratings and lower its borrowing costs over time, per the company’s regulatory filing.

Share conversion

Funds managed by Blackstone Private Equity will invest ₹5,000 crore through a preferential allotment of compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS). The CCPS will convert into equity shares at ₹602 per share within 18 months of allotment. On conversion, Blackstone will have about a 16 per cent stake in IIFL Finance.

Promoters Nirmal Jain and R Venkataraman will invest ₹1,264 crore through preferential warrants. Anchorage Capital will participate through a ₹150 crore primary equity investment.

The upside

The company said the funds will strengthen capital adequacy, improve cost of funds, accelerate growth in its gold finance business, and support its ambition to become one of India’s largest diversified retail NBFCs. Proposed appointment of Blackstone representatives to the board is expected to strengthen governance while preserving entrepreneurial culture. The proposed transaction is subject to customary regulatory and shareholder approvals.
