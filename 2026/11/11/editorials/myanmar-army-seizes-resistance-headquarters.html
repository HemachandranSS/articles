Myanmar Army seizes resistance headquarters, 10 opposition bases

Associated Press

BANGKOK  |  November 11, 2026

Myanmar’s military has seized a key resistance military command headquarters and 10 other bases along the border between Mon state and the Bago region, in the south of the country, state media reported on Tuesday, marking a new gain by the Army in the civil war as it seeks control of regions long held by resistance forces.

Key Terminologies

  1. Resistance Headquarters (எதிர்ப்புப் படைத் தலைமை)
  2. Mon State–Bago Border (மான்–பாகோ எல்லை)
  3. Opposition Bases (எதிர்க்கட்சி தளங்கள்)
  4. Civil War Territorial Gain (ஆட்சி முன்னேற்றம்)
