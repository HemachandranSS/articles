Explosions heard in Riyadh; coalition says missile intercepted

Agence France-Presse

RIYADH  |  November 11, 2026

Multiple explosions rang out over the Saudi capital Riyadh early on Tuesday, as the coalition fighting Yemen’s Houthis said it shot down a missile targeting the city, which has suffered weeks of disruptive attacks.

Residents received a mobile-phone alert urging them to shelter from an “aerial threat”. The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a ballistic missile. “Air defences intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile launched by the terrorist Houthi militia towards Riyadh,” the coalition posted on X.

The incident came two days after the Houthis (Ansar Allah) struck a psychological blow by hitting a skyscraper belonging to oil giant Aramco in Riyadh’s gleaming financial district. Saudi authorities had earlier said the building was hit by falling shrapnel after an “attempted attack”.

Saudi Arabia, the world’s biggest oil exporter, has faced near-daily Houthi strikes since early October, including deadly attacks that closed Riyadh airport for two weeks. Riyadh’s schoolchildren, after two days back in the classroom, resumed online learning on Tuesday “as part of the ongoing precautionary measures”.
