CITY MATTERS · CHENNAI · POLLUTION

Corporation to monitor air quality in city; installs sensors at 93 places

Digital boards have been put up to display real-time air quality index in 75 places; the Integrated Command and Control Centre is expected to play a central role in monitoring, analysing, coordinating, and managing air quality across the city

Aloysius Xavier Lopez

CHENNAI  |  November 11, 2026

The Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) is set to start real-time air quality monitoring at 75 locations, disseminating air quality data with the installation of on-site digital display boards.

The digital display boards will show real-time data in areas such as Thalankuppam, Ennore, Ernavoor, Andarkuppam, Chinnamanthur, Manjambakkam, Puzhal, Kathirvedu, Puthagaram, Chinnakodungaiyur, R.K. Nagar, Royapuram, Ripon Buildings, Thousand Lights, Kolathur, Korattur Lake, Padi, Villivakkam, Rajamangalam, Kilpauk, Shenoy Nagar, Nungambakkam, Triplicane, Koyambedu, Maduravoyal, Valasaravakkam, Adambakkam, Nanganallur, Velachery, Taramani, Palavakkam, Uthandi, and Chemmanchery.

Most of the digital display boards and air quality sensors have been installed on the premises of schools and hospitals of the GCC. Officials said sensors and boards were ready for display at 75 locations; a decision on display of real-time air quality index is expected shortly. The civic body has installed sensors at 93 places, but real-time display is likely to be launched only in 75 locations.

Planting of trees

Based on analytics, the civic body will also start inspection of polluted locations and check construction sites, make decisions about planting of trees in areas with poor air quality, and launch initiatives to increase the usage of public transport on polluted roads.

The Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) is expected to play a central role in monitoring, analysing, coordinating, and managing air quality across the city, tracking major pollutants through a centralised dashboard.

According to assessment of data by ICCC during Deepavali, the highest location averages of AQI was reported in Kallikuppam, Oragadam Ambattur, Valasaravakkam, Triplicane, and Ernavoor.
