Pentagon chief Hegseth calls move to impeach him a farce, sabotage

Reuters

WASHINGTON  |  November 11, 2026

U.S. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth on Tuesday called an effort to impeach him over abuse of power in the Iran war a farce and sabotage that plays into the hands of adversaries.

Mr. Hegseth made the comments a day after U.S. Representative Thomas Massie introduced eight articles of impeachment against him.
