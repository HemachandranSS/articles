Israeli forces shoot dead teen in West Bank, say Palestinian officials

Agence France-Presse

RAMALLAH  |  November 11, 2026

Israeli forces shot dead a 14-year-old in the occupied West Bank, Palestinian officials said, with Israel saying it had acted to “thwart stone-hurling and terrorist attacks” in the area.

Moutaz Eyad Daghlas was killed in Burqa, northwest of Nablus, late on Monday, the Palestinian Health Ministry said.
