Trilateral meet with Putin, Xi likely next week, says Trump

Ananth Krishnan

BEIJING  |  November 10, 2026

U.S. President Donald Trump has said he will likely meet Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in a rare trilateral leaders’ meeting on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) gathering in Shenzhen next week.

Beijing said on Monday it was “in communication with relevant parties” over the meeting, not ruling out the possibility as leaders gather for the APEC summit in the southern Chinese city on November 18 and 19.

Mr. Trump on Sunday indicated to presspersons that a deal on the Russia-Ukraine war, the Iran issue, and nuclear arms control would be on the agenda. “One of the meetings will be with President Putin and President Xi, and that will be interesting. We are talking a lot of things, including denuclearisation... We are trying to end the war in Iran,” Mr. Trump was quoted as saying.

Mr. Trump and Mr. Xi will meet bilaterally next week for the third time this year, following the U.S. President’s visit to Beijing in May and the Chinese President travelling to Washington in September.
