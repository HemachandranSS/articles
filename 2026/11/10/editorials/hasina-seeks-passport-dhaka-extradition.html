INDIA · BANGLADESH · DIPLOMACY

Hasina seeks new passport; Dhaka firm on extradition

London-based law firm seeks new documents for the ousted leader; Acting Foreign Minister of Bangladesh calls Hasina a ‘terrorist’, says India should hand her over under the extradition treaty.

Kallol Bhattacherjee

NEW DELHI  |  November 10, 2026

Hours after a legal team representing deposed Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina sought a new passport for her, the Government of Bangladesh asserted on Monday that Ms. Hasina can return to Bangladesh through an “acceptable prisoner exchange” programme under the India–Bangladesh extradition treaty.

A source in Dhaka told The Hindu that Ms. Hasina has requested the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, through a London-based legal firm, for a new passport and a National Identity Card. The official said the Ministry is verifying the letter and will take a decision after internal consultations.

Ms. Hasina’s passport was revoked after she escaped to India in August 2024 after an uprising toppled her government. She had announced in a press conference in August this year that she may return to her country in December despite facing serious legal challenges.

Speaking to the media in Dhaka, the country’s Acting Foreign Minister Humayun Kabir described Ms. Hasina as a “terrorist” who had fled to India after committing crimes.

“Bangladesh will seek the return of Sheikh Hasina through an acceptable prisoner exchange process.” — Humayun Kabir, Acting Foreign Minister

“Bangladesh will not issue a passport to any terrorist; there is no provision for such an action,” Mr. Kabir said, adding that India should hand over Ms. Hasina under the extradition treaty.

The External Affairs Ministry is yet to respond to Mr. Kabir’s remarks, but a source familiar with the Indian stance on the matter told The Hindu that the Bangladeshi position of not issuing a passport to Ms. Hasina will further complicate the matter, as a passport will be needed if India were to consider her extradition.
