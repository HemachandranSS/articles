Netanyahu’s poll rival Eisenkot tasked with forming govt. in Israel

Associated Press

JERUSALEM  |  November 10, 2026

Israeli President Isaac Herzog formally gave Opposition politician Gadi Eisenkot the first chance to form the next government on Sunday evening.

“I hope with all my heart that whatever government is formed will lead to unity, connection and healing among all segments of our people,” Mr. Herzog said.

Mr. Eisenkot has 28 days to form a coalition, with a possible two-week extension from the President. If he is unable to form a government, Benjamin Netanyahu could then be given a chance.

Mr. Eisenkot promised to give the country a leadership that people can trust, that will act in the interests of the public and that represents all parts of society.

“An absolute majority of Knesset members representing the Israeli public support change,” he said. “I intend to navigate the situation with patience, humility, composure and a deep commitment to a Jewish, equal, liberal, and secure Israel.”

The October 27 election gave neither his bloc nor Mr. Netanyahu’s a clear majority in Parliament. Among the possible kingmakers are the Arab-led parties.
