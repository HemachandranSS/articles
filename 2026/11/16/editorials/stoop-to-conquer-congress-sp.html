Stoop to conquer

Congress should scale down its ambition while seeking alliance with SP

Editorial Desk

Editorial  |  November 16, 2026

Ahead of the 2027 Uttar Pradesh Assembly polls, seat-sharing friction between the Samajwadi Party and Congress has resurfaced. Reports that Congress wants about 181 of 403 seats — roughly 45% — look overly ambitious against its 2022 record of a 2.33% vote share, while the SP won 111 seats with about 32%.

The 2024 Lok Sabha combine did better — 43 of 80 seats and a 43.2% vote share — and Congress won 6 of 17 seats it contested (about 44.2% in those seats). That still does not justify a near-half claim without a statewide organisation. A well-targeted hundred-odd seats would be more realistic than delays that hurt alliances in Bihar and Tamil Nadu.

Against the BJP’s hegemonic hold in the Hindi heartland, the editorial’s warning is blunt: swimming together beats sinking separately.
