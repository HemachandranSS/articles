PM in Manila: Giving new momentum to Act East Policy

Gurjit Singh

Op-Ed  |  November 16, 2026

Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s presence at the ASEAN-India and East Asia Summits in Manila on November 16–17 comes as the 2026 Year of Maritime Cooperation closes and the Dialogue Partnership nears 35 years (Comprehensive Strategic Partnership turns five in 2027).

India’s Act East Policy is not merely an outreach to Southeast Asia. It is increasingly about working with ASEAN to shape a stable, prosperous and inclusive Indo-Pacific.

ASEAN centrality, maritime drills (including MILAN 2026 and IFC-IOR information-sharing), the AITIGA goods-trade review, Digital Public Infrastructure and Project Nexus payments links, the Bali Scale Hub, and possible Quad optics in Manila all feed the same agenda. Trade near $128 billion last year, deeper ties with the Philippines chair and other ASEAN partners, BRICS outreach, and the 2026–2030 Plan of Action are meant to turn political warmth into tangible regional gains.

Key Terminologies

  1. Act East Policy / Indo-Pacific (ஆக்ட் ஈஸ்ட் கொள்கை / இந்தோ-பசிபிக்)
  2. AITIGA review (ஆசியான்-இந்தியா பொருள் வர்த்தக ஒப்பந்த மறுஆய்வு)
  3. Project Nexus / DPI (ப்ராஜெக்ட் நெக்சஸ் / டிஜிட்டல் பொது உள்கட்டமைப்பு)
  4. Quad / ASEAN centrality (குவாட் / ஆசியான் மையத்துவம்)
