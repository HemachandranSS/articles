Nvidia: a meteoric rise and uncertain future

Nitika Francis, Sambavi Parthasarathy & Pon Vasanth B.A.

Data Point  |  November 16, 2026

Nvidia’s climb to the first $4 trillion market capitalisation — and the sharp pullback after CEO Jensen Huang’s exit amid reported affairs with a client and a direct report — frames this Data Point on AI-chip dominance and succession risk.

Charts track market-cap leap from outside the top league to the most valued name in under three years; S&P 500 weight rising from $1.0 to $7.6 of every $100 by March 2026; data-centre revenue driving a more than 12-fold sales jump to $96.2 billion by Q2 FY27; stakes and financing totalling about $98.9 billion by July 2026; roughly 60% AI compute share under pressure from Google TPUs and AMD; and AMD closing Nvidia’s inference-performance lead to about 1.2× by September 2026.

Sources include U.S. SEC filings, YCharts, Epoch AI, MLPerf and MLCommons. The company’s next chapter hinges on finding a successor and whether rivals erode its AI computing franchise.
