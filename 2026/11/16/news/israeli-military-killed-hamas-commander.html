Israeli military says it killed top Hamas commander

Agence France-Presse

JERUSALEM  |  November 16, 2026

The Israeli military said on Sunday that it and the Shin Bet security agency had struck Samir Abu Younis, a senior commander in Hamas’s military wing who they said played a central role in planning and supervising the October 7, 2023 attacks on Israel.

Hamas’s Al-Qassam Brigade confirmed his death on Telegram, calling it a “cowardly assassination operation” carried out on Saturday. A joint IDF–ISA statement said they had “struck a senior terrorist in Hamas’ military wing.”
