Yemeni forces retreat from Bab el-Mandeb Strait held by Houthis

Reuters

SANAA  |  November 16, 2026

Yemeni government forces announced on Sunday a withdrawal from positions near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait to focus on defending their main base in Aden, a major setback for the Saudi-backed campaign against the Houthis.

The Southern Armed Forces called the move a “redeployment” from Dhubab Airport and the Bab el-Mandeb area, ending a six-week offensive to reclaim the Red Sea coast seized by the Houthis in a September advance that disrupted oil exports.

Priority is now securing Aden and neighbouring governorates. Eight people were killed and 12 wounded in a Houthi missile attack in Lahj province near Aden on Sunday. The retreat raises fears of further Houthi advances toward Aden and underscores Saudi difficulties in stopping drone and missile attacks.
