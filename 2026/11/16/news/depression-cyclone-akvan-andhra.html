Depression likely to turn into cyclone, move towards A.P.

The Hindu Bureau

VIJAYAWADA/CHENNAI  |  November 16, 2026

A well-marked low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal has intensified into a depression and is likely to strengthen into a very severe cyclonic storm with gusts up to 145 kmph, the meteorological department said.

The RMC, Chennai said the system may become a cyclonic storm over the south and adjoining west-central Bay on Monday, then a severe cyclonic storm on Tuesday while moving northwest towards the Andhra Pradesh coast.

If Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Andhra Pradesh, it will be the third cyclone within a month to hit the State after Arnab and Murasu. Moderate rain is likely in north coastal Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal on Monday, with heavy rain in Chennai on Tuesday and heavy to very heavy rain on Wednesday.
