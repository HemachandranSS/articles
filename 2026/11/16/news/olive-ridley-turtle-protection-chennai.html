Forest Dept. initiates measures to protect Olive Ridley turtles this nesting season

The Hindu Bureau

CHENNAI  |  November 16, 2026

The Tamil Nadu Forest Department has begun conservation measures along the Chennai coast ahead of the Olive Ridley turtle migratory and nesting season expected from December.

The Chennai Wildlife Division started beach and boat patrols in November under SOPs — two to three hours, three to four times a week from Marina to Neelankarai and Uthandi, with similar operations in the Pulicat range, focusing on vulnerable nesting stretches, demarcation, beach cleaning and obstacle removal.

Volunteers will be trained in nest identification and reporting. The Marine Elite Force, with Fisheries, is monitoring trawlers to enforce fishing restrictions that reduce accidental turtle deaths in nets.
