‘It would have been better had BJP not come to power’

BJP MLA Ratna Debnath alleged the role of turncoat Trinamool leaders in worker’s killing

Shiv Sahay Singh

KOLKATA  |  November 16, 2026

BJP MLA Ratna Debnath of Panihati said it would have been better had the BJP not come to power in West Bengal, after party worker Arup Saha died of injuries following a scuffle.

She alleged “turncoat” former Trinamool leaders who joined after the May 4 Assembly results were behind internal violence, claiming the district president appointed mostly ex-Trinamool ward conveners in Panihati. The clash began when Arup’s wife Ruma, a mandal secretary, was abused while protesting an attack on a businessman.

Similar factional feuds were reported in Barrackpore, where MLA Kaustav Bagchi faced “go back” slogans over distributing Chhath Puja sarees with a man accused in a BJP leader’s murder.
