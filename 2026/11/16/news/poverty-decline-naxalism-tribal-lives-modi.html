Poverty decline, end of Naxalism improved tribal lives: PM

The Hindu Bureau

NEW DELHI  |  November 16, 2026

Quoting a World Bank report that nearly 50 crore Indians had escaped extreme poverty, and claiming the country had been freed from Naxalism, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said both milestones had improved living conditions for tribal communities.

Addressing a Gadchiroli gathering via videoconference on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas — the 151st birth anniversary of Birsa Munda — he laid foundation stones for 246 projects worth about ₹3,000 crore.

He said poverty reduction over 12–13 years was driven by society’s collective strength and targeted Central tribal welfare schemes. On Naxalism, he recalled that nearly 125 districts were affected in 2014, with tribal communities among the worst hit by violence.
