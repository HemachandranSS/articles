Weather Watch: city temperatures, rainfall and air quality

INSAT/IMD satellite outlook, Monday forecast, and pollutant readings across major cities

The Hindu Bureau

CHENNAI  |  November 16, 2026

Monday’s Weather Watch shows thunderstorms, lightning and gusty winds likely over the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and dense fog in isolated places over Uttar Pradesh, with INSAT/IMD imagery highlighting Bay of Bengal cloud cover.

Chennai recorded 0.3 mm rain with a max of 28.7°C and min of 24.6°C. Among metros, Delhi’s AQI was in the poor band (PM2.5 237), while Chennai was moderate (PM2.5 117). Muzaffarnagar logged an unhealthy AQI of 338; Aizawl a healthy 22.
