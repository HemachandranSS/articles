Bengal police gun down man accused of shooting at constable

Fifth instance of an accused being killed by police within 6 months of BJP coming to power in State; civil society group slams ‘extrajudicial execution’

Shiv Sahay Singh

KOLKATA  |  November 16, 2026

West Bengal police killed Ratan Sheikh, accused of shooting at constable Biswajit Das in Bolpur, during an operation on Sunday at a brick kiln in Nanoor, Birbhum — the fifth such police killing within six months of the BJP forming the State government.

Police said Sheikh opened fire while trying to evade arrest. Chief Minister Suvendu Adhikari defended the action, asking whether police should “offer sweets” if fired upon, echoing campaign rhetoric that those accused of heinous crimes would be dealt with swiftly.

The Association for Protection of Democratic Rights condemned an “extrajudicial execution,” with vice-president Ranajit Sur alleging Sheikh was “captured and then killed,” accusing the government of belittling the judiciary and the Constitution. Earlier encounters were reported in Baruipur, Gaighata, Shamsherganj and Raiganj.
