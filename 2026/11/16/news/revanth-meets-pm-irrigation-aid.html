Revanth meets PM, seeks aid for irrigation projects

Telangana CM urges Modi to expedite clearances, and appeals for Centre’s help to secure approval from Maharashtra for Tummidihatti project; he invites PM to attend the Khelo India Youth Games

R. Ravikanth Reddy

HYDERABAD  |  November 16, 2026

Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy met Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi on Sunday and sought an additional ₹5,000 crore under the Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment to complete irrigation projects.

He asked the Centre to help secure Maharashtra’s nod for a barrage at Tummidihatti on the Pranahita at 152 metres height, with compensation for submerged land, vital for irrigation and drinking water in backward tribal regions.

He also sought faster clearances for the Palamuru-Rangareddy Lift Irrigation Scheme, Sitarama Sagar Multipurpose Project and Dindi Lift Irrigation Scheme, and invited the PM to the Khelo India Youth Games-2026 in Hyderabad in December. Union Minister Mansukh Mandaviya and State Ministers N. Uttam Kumar Reddy and Vakiti Srihari attended.

Key Terminologies

  1. Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment (மாநிலங்களுக்கான சிறப்பு மூலதன உதவித் திட்டம்)
  2. Pranahita / Tummidihatti (பிரணஹிதா / தும்மிடிஹட்டி)
  3. Khelo India Youth Games-2026 (கேலோ இந்தியா இளைஞர் விளையாட்டு-2026)
  4. Palamuru-Rangareddy LIS (பலமூரு-ரங்காரெட்டி உயர்த்தி பாசனம்)
