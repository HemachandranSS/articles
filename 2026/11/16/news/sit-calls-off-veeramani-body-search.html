SIT calls off search for body of victim at Veeramani’s house

The police say they found no trace of the body alleged to have been buried there; however, investigators say they will continue to pursue the information shared by one of the survivors

S. Vijay Kumar

CHENNAI  |  November 16, 2026

The Special Investigation Team ended an intensive excavation search at Gem Granites owner R. Veeramani’s Royapettah residence after finding no trace of a body a survivor said she had seen buried there years ago.

Police said they will still pursue the survivor’s information. Veeramani faces nine POCSO cases and was earlier booked in a 2004 case of abetment of suicide of a minor. Ten people are in Puzhal Central Prison; statements from 13 survivors have been recorded; four videos and driver Bala alias Perumal have been secured.
