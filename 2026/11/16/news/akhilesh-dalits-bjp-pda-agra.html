Dalits will pull the rug out from under BJP’s feet, says SP chief

Akhilesh urges party workers to stay vigilant against electoral manipulation during special summary revision in U.P.; claims the BJP is afraid because Dalits had successfully registered their votes and Muslims had safeguarded theirs during the SIR

Anuj Kumar

GHAZIABAD  |  November 16, 2026

Samajwadi Party president Akhilesh Yadav told a Dalit Sammelan in Agra that Dalits would “pull the rug out from under the BJP’s feet,” coining the slogan “Save vote, save Constitution.”

He urged vigilance against “vote ki hera pheri” during the special summary revision, claiming the BJP feared Dalits who had registered votes and Muslims who safeguarded theirs in the SIR. He redefined PDA as Pichhde, Dumdar Dalit and Alpasankhyak, and also as Peedit, Dukhi and Apmanit.

Promising rule of law and social justice if voted to power in the 2027 U.P. Assembly polls, he pledged an SC/ST welfare package and legal protection for Ambedkar statues, dismissed AIMIM–Azad Samaj Party allies as “siyasat ke sailani,” and said the manifesto is due on November 26.

Key Terminologies

  1. Akhilesh Yadav / Samajwadi Party (அகிலேஷ் யாதவ் / சமாஜ்வாதி கட்சி)
  2. Save vote, save Constitution (வாக்கை காப்பாற்று, அரசியலமைப்பை காப்பாற்று)
  3. Dumdar Dalit (வலிமையான தலித்)
  4. U.P. Assembly 2027 (உ.பி. சட்டப்பேரவை 2027)
