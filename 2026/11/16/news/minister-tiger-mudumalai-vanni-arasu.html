Minister faces flak for pursuing tiger in Mudumalai reserve

The Hindu Bureau

UDHAGAMANDALAM  |  November 16, 2026

Conservationists demanded action against Social Justice Minister Vanni Arasu after a social media video showed him pursuing a tiger in the Mudumalai Tiger Reserve and approaching a wild elephant in Bandipur.

They urged the Forest Department to register a case under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972, arguing the Minister set a bad precedent by flouting rules that bar tourists from approaching wildlife or alighting from safari vehicles.

A senior Forest official said the tiger was spotted on private resort property in Achakarai, that the Minister had not entered reserve forest or alighted from a safari vehicle, and that staff were present for safety. Conservationists rejected the explanation and sought action against staff who failed to stop the approach.
