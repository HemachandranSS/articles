China+1 is no longer a trend, it’s an operational imperative: Singhania

PLAN B. US a key export market; tariff uncertainty prompting expansion in the UK, Europe: Raymond Group CMD

Suresh P Iyengar · Mumbai

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

In a BL.interview, Raymond Group CMD Gautam Hari Singhania frames the reorganised, net debt-free group — Raymond Lifestyle, Raymond Realty and the engineering/precision platform (including JK Files and Maini Precision) — around professional boards and China+1 manufacturing demand.

My role as a promoter is not to have all the answers. It is to build teams that can take the organisation further than any one individual could. — GAUTAM HARI SINGHANIA

H1 FY27 portfolio EBITDA rose 15% to ₹792 crore; engineering and specialty steel are about 49% of earnings. Aerospace (up 26% in FY26), defence and auto/EV parts lead engineering growth; lifestyle targets casual wear at ~25% of the mix; realty stays MMR-focused and asset-light. The US remains a key export market while tariff risk pushes UK/Europe expansion (clients such as Charles Tyrwhitt and Ted Baker). Aerospace and auto components could later become separate listed identities at scale.

Key Terminologies

  1. China+1 operational imperative (சீனா+1 செயல்பாட்டுத் தேவை)
  2. Portfolio EBITDA / demerger path (போர்ட்ஃபோலியோ EBITDA / பிரிப்புப் பாதை)
  3. Aerospace / auto-EV components (விண்வெளி / ஆட்டோ-ஈவி பாகங்கள்)
  4. Asset-light MMR realty (சொத்து-இலேசான மும்பை ரியல்டி)
