Digital dependencies

A diagnosis of India’s fragile tech sovereignty

Yazhini MV

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

Yazhini MV reviews Anand V. and Srinath Sridharan’s Power of UncertAInty (Rupa, ₹395), which frames sovereignty as the capacity to act with available tools and own the consequences. India’s weak spot, they argue, is the gap between gifted individuals and thin institutional capability across KYC, UPI crime, industrial policy, AI and chips.

On AI, overseas hardware and fabs still dominate; IndiaAI’s tens of thousands of GPUs buy access, not control of the stack. Regulatory gaps around digital payments feed fraud networks. The diagnosis of hidden dependencies is sharp; the prescriptions — stronger industrial policy and institutions — feel familiar, with answers less distinctive than the questions.
