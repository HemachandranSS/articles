Still stuck on promises, not delivery

WHO’S PAYING? The ongoing UN climate meet in Turkiye has so far failed to grapple with the toughest questions on transition funding

Soumya Sarkar

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

From Antalya’s COP31, Soumya Sarkar finds clean-power and efficient-building announcements but little grip on who pays for the transition. Türkiye wants electricity’s share of global energy up from 23% to 35% by 2035; finance remains the bottleneck after Baku’s $300 billion / $1.3 trillion tracks.

A November 13 text still jams on fossil fuels, what counts as climate finance, and rich-country duties. For India the stakes are concrete: power, transport and climate resilience without sacrificing growth. The underfunded Loss and Damage window — Nepal’s $20 million versus a $4.7 billion need — shows why week two must lock a credible delivery path, not more pledges.

Key Terminologies

  1. Transition funding / who pays (மாற்ற நிதி / யார் செலுத்துவது)
  2. Climate finance definition fight (காலநிலை நிதி வரையறைப் போட்டி)
  3. Electrification target 2035 (மின்மயமாக்கல் இலக்கு 2035)
  4. Loss and Damage underfunding (இழப்பு-சேத நிதிப் பற்றாக்குறை)
