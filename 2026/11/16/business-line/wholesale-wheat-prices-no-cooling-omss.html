Wholesale wheat prices show no signs of cooling

Traders and experts say govt should take corrective action as FCI auction rates do not reflect in the market despite weekly sales of 2 lt

Prabhudatta Mishra · New Delhi

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

Wholesale wheat keeps firm even after weekly OMSS auctions of about 2 lakh tonnes. Traders say cheaper FCI auction rates are not flowing through to wholesale. October CPI hit 6.09% (food 8.94%; cereal category 12.51% vs wheat ~3.05%).

Auction clearing prices have eased in several States while successful bidder counts jumped (e.g. Punjab 204→325; Delhi 35→66). Traders allege some buyers register as processors to lift grain and resell at higher market rates — calling for tighter corrective action.
