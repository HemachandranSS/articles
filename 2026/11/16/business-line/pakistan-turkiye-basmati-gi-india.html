Pakistan gets GI tag for its basmati from Türkiye, but India stands to gain too

Subramani Ra Mancombu · Chennai

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

Pakistan secured a Turkish Geographical Indication (mahrec İşareti) for basmati from TURKPATENT on February 1, 2026, after filing in June 2024. India’s pending bid is under Menşe Adı — a protected designation of origin (PDO) filed July 31, 2025 — which needs a scientific environment–product link.

A January 2026 Pakistani amendment dropped “Azad Kashmir” from the description — read as a diplomatic win for India. Türkiye’s wide regional list (Sindh, Balochistan, KPK) may also bolster Madhya Pradesh’s claim to be treated as a basmati-growing region in Indian litigation before the Madras High Court (hearing November 17) and related Supreme Court proceedings involving APEDA and the Patent Office.

Key Terminologies

  1. GI / Protected Designation of Origin (புவியியல் குறியீடு / பாதுகாக்கப்பட்ட தோற்றப் பெயர்)
  2. TURKPATENT / mahrec İşareti (டர்க்பேட்டண்ட் / துருக்கி ஜிஐ)
  3. APEDA / basmati region dispute (அபேடா / பாஸ்மதி பகுதி பிரச்சினை)
  4. Madhya Pradesh GI claim (மத்தியப் பிரதேச ஜிஐ கோரிக்கை)
