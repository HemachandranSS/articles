Commerce, Textiles Ministries make case for extension of export schemes

Amiti Sen · New Delhi

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

Commerce and Textiles Ministries are pressing the Department of Revenue to extend RoDTEP and apparel-focused RoSCTL, both recently rolled only three months in September and due to lapse on December 31, 2026.

Industry wants a 3–5 year runway for negotiating certainty. Commerce has sought RoDTEP outlay of about ₹23,000 crore for 2026–27 versus a ₹10,000 crore budget estimate, citing merchandise exports up 21.96% to $309 billion in April–October 2026–27. Impact notes aim to show benefits across sectors; Finance remains cautious pending proof the zero-rating schemes work as intended.
