The emerging new dispute landscape in India’s renewable energy sector

Beyond just tariffs, developers today face litigation related to factors ranging from law changes to habitat constraints

Kumar Shashwat Singh Sawno & Kartikey Trivedi

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

Renewable litigation has moved past tariff and PPA fights under the Electricity Act, 2003. Courts have backed carrying costs on restitutionary principles for change-in-law shocks, and held that commercial curtailment of must-run RE is impermissible without compensation.

Habitat cases add another layer: the Supreme Court’s 2021 undergrounding directions to protect the Great Indian Bustard in Rajasthan and Gujarat — later recalibrated in 2024 — stalled projects and show environment risk is now central. The authors urge automatic change-in-law compensation, auditable dispatch data for curtailment, and time-bound single-window forest/wildlife clearances.

Key Terminologies

  1. PPA / Electricity Act tariffs (மின் கொள்முதல் ஒப்பந்தம் / மின்சாரச் சட்டக் கட்டணம்)
  2. Change-in-law / carrying cost (சட்ட மாற்றம் / சுமந்து செல்லும் செலவு)
  3. Must-run / commercial curtailment (கட்டாய இயக்கம் / வணிகக் குறைப்பு)
  4. Great Indian Bustard habitat litigation (பெரிய இந்தியன் பஸ்டர்ட் வாழிட வழக்கு)
