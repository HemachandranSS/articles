COP out

Developed world must pick up the tab on climate finance

Editorial Desk

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

At COP31 in Antalya, some 190 countries face the same gap: about 160 Parties have filed NDC 3.0 pledges, but credible money is scarce. UNEP’s Limiting Overshoot warns 1.5°C will be breached within years.

Baku’s deal — at least $300 billion a year from wealthy nations by 2035, and $1.3 trillion from all sources — still splits public vs private burden. Adaptation finance was only about $28–32 billion in 2024 against a $305 billion need by 2035; the Loss and Damage Fund remains thin (Nepal’s $20 million vs a $4.7 billion recovery bill). Türkiye’s push to lift electricity’s energy share from 23% to 35% by 2035 looks underfunded. The editorial’s ask is blunt: the developed world must pick up the tab.

Key Terminologies

  1. COP31 / NDC 3.0 (காப்31 / தேசிய பங்களிப்பு 3.0)
  2. Climate finance / $300 bn track (காலநிலை நிதி / $300 பில்லியன் பாதை)
  3. Adaptation Gap Report (தகவமைப்பு இடைவெளி அறிக்கை)
  4. Loss and Damage Fund (இழப்பு மற்றும் சேத நிதி)
