CAG flags lapses in BIS gold hallmarking checks

BIS falls short of surveillance audit target for 83% of 2.3 lakh jewellers

Dalip Singh · New Delhi

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

A draft CAG ‘Subject Specific Compliance Audit’ on the BIS finds weak oversight of hallmarked gold — potentially shaking consumer confidence in purity claims.

Between 2020–21 and now, BIS missed at least one annual surveillance visit for 83.25% of registered jewellers. Of about 2.3 lakh registered shops, 2024–25 alone saw 28,865 registrations but only 5,444 audits (23,421 or 81.13% unchecked). Hallmarking is mandatory in 392 districts via Assaying and Hallmarking Centres and a unique six-character HUID; AHC surveillance also fell short by about 43.7%.

BIS blamed manpower gaps and remote sampling; auditors were unconvinced. Consumers can check HUIDs on the BIS Care app, but without routine checks, “value for money” remains hard to verify. The report is expected in Parliament after the Ministry’s final reply.
