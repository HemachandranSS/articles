Consent manager portal under DPDP Act goes live, but key questions remain

Sindhu Hariharan · Chennai

The Hindu Business Line  |  November 16, 2026

MeitY has opened a portal for firms to register as consent managers under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act — intermediaries that help people manage personal-data consent — but the Data Protection Board that must evaluate applications is still not constituted.

Core issues: no Data Protection Board yet; unclear interoperability; and who pays for consent management.

Industry voices (IQWorks, Deloitte, Redacto, Privy/IDfy) flag missing technical standards, hard interoperability, and unsettled economics. Officials say DPB appointments are “soon.” Businesses are told not to wait for consent managers — use is optional for now — and to treat the May 2027 compliance deadline as fixed.

Key Terminologies

  1. DPDP Act / consent manager (டிபிடிபி சட்டம் / ஒப்புதல் மேலாளர்)
  2. Data Protection Board (DPB) (தரவுப் பாதுகாப்பு வாரியம்)
  3. Interoperability standards (இணைசெயல்பாட்டுத் தரங்கள்)
  4. May 2027 compliance deadline (மே 2027 இணக்கக் கெடு)
