Equity vs bonds

Concentrated bets in equity market could generate high returns if your bet turns right; on the other hand, diversification pays off for bond investments

Venkatesh Bangaruswamy

CHENNAI  |  November 16, 2026

Diversification has virtues — and so do concentrated bets such as sector funds — but the logic of concentration differs sharply between equity and bond markets.

Equity has theoretically unlimited upside; a concentrated call, such as a banking sector ETF if you expect banks to rally, can pay off though it remains risky within a finite investment horizon.

Bonds are obligations whose prices are the present value of future interest cash flows and move inversely with interest rates. With rates floored but able to rise with inflation, bond downside often exceeds upside. A single issuer default can spook a whole sector’s credit spread. So while concentrated equity bets can deliver high returns when right, bond investors need diversification across sectors and maturities to manage credit and interest-rate risk.

Key Terminologies

  1. Equity upside vs bond obligations (பங்கு ஏற்றம் எதிர் பத்திரக் கடப்பாடுகள்)
  2. Sector funds / ETFs (துறை நிதிகள் / ஈடிஎஃப்)
  3. Interest-rate risk (வட்டி விகித இடர்)
  4. Credit risk / diversification (கடன் இடர் / பன்முகப்படுத்தல்)
