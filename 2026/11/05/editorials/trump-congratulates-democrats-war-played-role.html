Trump congratulates Democrats on result, says war played role

V-P Vance echoes Trump’s remark on reason for loss

Agence France-Presse

WASHINGTON  |  November 5, 2026

President Donald Trump offered complimentary words to Democrats after a Republican setback in midterm elections, saying the Iran war upended GOP prospects while refusing personal blame.

The conflict and oil-price surge were “vastly unpopular,” analysts said — a stalled conflict that Vice-President J.D. Vance, a critic of foreign interventionism, distanced himself from ahead of a possible 2028 White House bid. Vance blamed the war for the loss in a post on X.

Trump said he was ready to work with Democrats who now control Congress in a divided government but would wield executive power to veto disliked bills — returning them with a “nice stamp – ‘veto.’” On Truth Social he still claimed a personal ballot would have won in a landslide. A political pundit debate turned on whether war costs were justified given domestic affordability anger.
