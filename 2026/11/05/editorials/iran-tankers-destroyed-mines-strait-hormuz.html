Iran says tankers destroyed after hitting mines in strait

Agence France-Presse

TEHRAN  |  November 5, 2026

Four oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines while attempting the Strait of Hormuz, Iran said Wednesday — a day after Donald Trump said the U.S. had struck the Islamic republic following reports of a Qeshm raid.

The IRGC said four “non-compliant” tankers were destroyed in a prohibited southern passage; two other vessels were halted. Guards also shot down a drone over the conduit Tehran claims to control, targeting ships without its authorisation since fighting escalated.

Finger on trigger

Indirect contact via mediators continued with little sign of a diplomatic breakthrough. Tehran says it may reopen Hormuz only if Washington accepts long-standing demands. Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf repeated that Iran was “ready for battle with its finger on the trigger,” while President Masoud Pezeshkian pressed dialogue: “No one will benefit from this war” if fighting resumes.
