U.S. slaps new sanctions on Indian firms for aiding Iran

Reuters

WASHINGTON  |  November 5, 2026

The U.S. Treasury issued fresh sanctions Wednesday against companies and individuals in India and Türkiye for aiding Tehran, warning it would pursue anyone backing the Iranian government.

The designations are part of Operation Economic Outcast, aimed at cutting Iran’s ability to sell oil and fund its war with the U.S. and Israel. Treasury said last week its actions were decimating Iran’s economy — raising secondary sanctions risk for Indian banks and shippers, and testing Delhi’s balancing act between energy ties, Gulf security, and compliance with Washington’s Iran crackdown.
