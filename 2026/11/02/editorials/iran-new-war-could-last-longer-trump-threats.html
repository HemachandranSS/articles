Iran says new war could last longer after Trump threats

The U.S. President would not be in a position to dictate the timing or conditions of its conclusion, says Tehran; Revolutionary Guards add that there would be ‘no geographic limitations’ in conflict.

Agence France-Presse

TEHRAN  |  November 2, 2026

Iran warned Sunday that any new war with the United States could last longer than prior bombardments, after President Donald Trump threatened further strikes and said Washington would “take care” of additional targets.

Army spokesperson Mohammad Akraminia said Tehran would not let the U.S. dictate when fighting ends or on what terms peace is offered. Revolutionary Guards spokesperson Hossein Mohebbi said there would be no geographic limitations to retaliation — signalling that the theatre of war might widen across the region.

Officials referenced diplomatic efforts by neighbours but insisted Iran’s response would outlast any short punitive campaign. References to Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei framed continuity of policy despite external pressure.
