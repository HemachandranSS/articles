Terrorism is ‘red line’, Pak. tells Afghanistan as it warns of action

Afghan Taliban had accused Pakistan of being unwilling to resolve tensions through dialogue

Press Trust of India

ISLAMABAD  |  November 2, 2026

Pakistan warned Afghanistan that it reserves the right to take all necessary measures against cross-border terror, saying terrorism is a red line and national security is non-negotiable.

In a Saturday night statement, the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting accused the Afghan Taliban of refusing to curb militancy emanating from Afghan soil and of blaming Islamabad for strained bilateral ties.

“Pakistan’s position is unequivocal: Terrorism is a red line and the security of its citizens is non-negotiable,” the Ministry said, responding to Taliban claims that Pakistan was unwilling to resolve tensions through dialogue. Islamabad alleges that TTP factions use Afghan territory to attack inside Pakistan.
