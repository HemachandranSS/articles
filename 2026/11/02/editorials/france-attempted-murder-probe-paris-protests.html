France launches ‘attempted murder’ probe over attacks

Agence France-Presse

PARIS  |  November 2, 2026

France opened an attempted murder investigation on Sunday after petrol bombs were thrown at security forces during Paris protests over soaring living costs.

Clashes erupted in Paris and other cities during a third weekend of mass demonstrations. The Paris police prefecture said several hundred rioters attacked officers with projectiles. Interior Minister Laurent Nunez called the violence “extraordinary.” Footage showed a home-made incendiary device exploding at an officer’s feet, engulfing his lower body in flames.

Prosecutors are investigating “attempted murder of persons vested with public authority.” Explosive devices were reportedly thrown at about 15 gendarmes as well as police officers — raising stakes for state monopoly on force amid Europe-wide unrest over affordability.
