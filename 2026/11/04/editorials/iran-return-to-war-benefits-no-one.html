Iran President says return to war benefits ‘no one’ after U.S. strikes

The Qeshm attack comes just hours after Trump signalled a deal with Iran ‘could happen’; Tehran’s Deputy Foreign Minister Gharibabadi says Iran retained the right to ‘legitimate self-defence’; Qatar says it remained hopeful of an interim deal in war

Agence France-Presse

TEHRAN  |  November 4, 2026

President Masoud Pezeshkian said Tuesday that a return to war would benefit “no one”, hours after reported U.S. strikes on Qeshm island in the Strait of Hormuz — a key flashpoint that has upended global markets.

Chief negotiator Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said Iran was “ready for battle with its finger on the trigger”; Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi cited legitimate self-defence after the strikes. Pezeshkian insisted on dialogue, noting the Supreme Leader had granted negotiators powers over issues once “off-limits.”

Defensive strikes

U.S. outlets described the Qeshm attacks as defensive. Israel’s military told AFP it was not involved. Trump posted that a settlement “could happen”; IRNA reported five blasts. Qatar, a protracted indirect negotiator, still hoped for an interim deal as Americans voted in midterm elections.
