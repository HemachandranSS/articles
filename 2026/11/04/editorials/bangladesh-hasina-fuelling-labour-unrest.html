Bangladesh accuses Hasina of fuelling labour unrest

Press Trust of India

DHAKA  |  November 4, 2026

Dhaka accused deposed Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina of inciting violent garment-worker unrest entering a fourth day, forcing hundreds of factories to halt and drawing extra paramilitary deployment.

The BGMEA said violence shut another 300 plants, bringing the total near 1,000 since October 31. Protests spread across Dhaka, Gazipur, Ashulia, Mirpur, Narayanganj and Mymensingh. Workers seek a minimum wage of 30,000 taka (about ₹23,000), more than double 12,500 taka, citing lost purchasing power as living costs rise.

Officials contrasted earlier assertions that Hasina and the disbanded Awami League were “politically irrelevant” with new allegations that external political networks stoke street clashes — a sign labour economics and succession politics remain fused in post-coup Bangladesh.
