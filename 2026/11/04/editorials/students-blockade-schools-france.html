Students blockade dozens of high schools, universities across France

Agence France-Presse

PARIS  |  November 4, 2026

Students blockaded dozens of high schools and universities Tuesday to press education reforms as trade unions prepared a nationwide strike.

After autumn break, protests resumed for renovations of dilapidated schools, smaller classes and more university places. Officials counted 48 of 3,700 lycées blocked; the USL union claimed 500. Fifty-one of 184 higher-education sites faced disruption.

USL denounced heavy-handed tactics — youths “pinned against walls” and riot units outside campuses. Education Minister Edouard Geffray said blocking schools deprives pupils of a fundamental right to study before exams. Thousands marched in Paris and other cities, linking student encroachment fears with Thursday’s union action.
