U.S. will stop $4 billion annual security assistance to Israel, announces Trump

Associated Press

WASHINGTON  |  November 4, 2026

President Donald Trump said the U.S. will end about $4 billion in annual security assistance to Israel from 2027 but continue arms sales — telling Fox News on Election Day that Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu broached the idea and that Israel “doesn’t need” the cash.

Trump framed flows as “barter” — “military aid or cash” — while noting aid has become a Democratic talking point, citing critics such as Michigan Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed. Far-right commentators including Tucker Carlson have also questioned the ally relationship.

In 2016 Barack Obama signed an unprecedented MOU for $38 billion over ten years ($3.8 billion yearly), gradually requiring all funds to buy U.S. hardware as Israeli domestic spending was phased out. Analysts called the announcement a last-ditch effort unlikely to reshape midterm outcomes.
