Ex-Maldives Foreign Minister arrested on sexual abuse charges

Meera Srinivasan

November 4, 2026

Former Maldivian Foreign Minister Abdulla Shahid was arrested late Monday over allegations of sexually abusing a minor, police said. He denies the charges and was detained around 10 p.m. on a court order.

Mr. Shahid is a prominent presidential aspirant within the main Opposition Maldivian Democratic Party (MDP) for 2028. Recently in New Delhi, he criticised President Mohamed Muizzu’s Pakistan visit and accused him of steering the Maldives in an anti-India direction — a core international relations fault line in Male.

In 2021 he received the Order of the Distinguished Rule of Izzuddin, the Maldives’ highest state honour. The arrest now intersects domestic criminal law with regional diplomacy narratives India watches closely. (With PTI inputs.)
