Aadhaar verification for Tatkal train tickets not violative of privacy rights, says Kerala HC

The High Court rules that optional biometric-linked OTP authentication prevents bulk ticket hoarding by syndicates and touts.

BY THE HINDU BUREAU

KOCHI  |  Constitutional Law, Governance & Digital Infrastructure

Aadhaar authentication required for Tatkal train ticketing system does not violate the Right to Privacy of a user, said Kerala High Court on Thursday.

The Division Bench of Chief Justice Soumen Sen and Justice V.M. Syam Kumar was considering a public interest litigation petition challenging the requirement of Aadhaar-based OTP authentication in the Tatkal ticketing system introduced by the Ministry of Railways.

The court observed that the authentication is optional and there is no compulsion to share the Aadhaar details for ticket purchases online through the Indian Railways Catering and Tourism Corporation website. It noted that the requirement for authentication for Tatkal tickets was justified based on experiences of alleged deceit, manipulation and bulk booking, which denied access to the ticketing system for genuine users.

The Ministry of Railways, Centre for Railway Information Systems, and IRCTC filed their statements, which explained that the rationale behind the policy is to ensure that only genuine individual users can access the Tatkal booking system. The provision is intended to prevent the bulk booking of tickets by touts, syndicates, and automated software.

Alternative suggestion

The court suggested that PAN card could be alternatively used for such verification, since there is OTP generation linked to PAN details and it would produce similar results to Aadhaar verification.
