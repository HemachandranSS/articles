Russia warns the U.S. against ‘unacceptable’ arms sales to Ukraine

Sergei Lavrov meets U.S. counterpart Marco Rubio on the sidelines of the ASEAN summit in Manila, while Volodymyr Zelensky engages American envoys to revive stalled peace negotiations.

BY AGENCE FRANCE-PRESSE

MANILA  |  Multilateral Diplomacy, Geopolitical Strategy & Defense Policy

Russia’s Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov warned Washington against making “unacceptable” arms deliveries to Ukraine in a Thursday meeting with U.S. counterpart Marco Rubio in Manila in Philippines, Moscow said.

Mr. Lavrov told the U.S. secretary of State “it was unacceptable to continue supplying weapons to the Kyiv regime”, the Russian Foreign Ministry said in a statement.

In their meeting held on the sidelines of a summit of Foreign Ministers of Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), Mr. Lavrov condemned “a destabilising policy by European countries, which are still seeking to inflict a ‘strategic defeat’ on Russia”.

He reiterated that the Russian side was ready for a “political-diplomatic resolution to the conflict”.

On Thursday, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said spoke with U.S. envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in a bid to revive stalled talks aimed at ending the war.
